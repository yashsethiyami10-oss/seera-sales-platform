# Phase 10 Requirements

Phase 10 is limited to governed analytics, reporting, automation, notifications and deterministic intelligence. It preserves portal, commercial, ledger, inventory, attribution, language and lifecycle truth frozen in Phases 1–9. MUV and the Seera production database are excluded.

Acceptance covers six separate dashboards, zero-filled month series, booked/delivered separation, scoped exports, bilingual notifications, configurable deduplicated rules, explainable recommendations, backend RBAC and a guarded TEST-only migration.
