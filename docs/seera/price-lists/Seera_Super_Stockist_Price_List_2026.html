Commercial Terms & Notes

Applicable to all orders placed against this price list

01

Validity

Rates are effective from 01 October 2026 and remain valid until superseded by a revised price list issued by the Company. This edition supersedes price list SEERA/SS-PL/2026/01 and all earlier Super Stockist price communications.

02

Taxes — Seera, Yuva, Shine Plus & Aatmanirbhar

Net Landing is the final invoice value per Box / Bag, inclusive of GST @ 18% (HSN 3402). Basic Rate and GST are shown as the tax break-up of that value.

03

MUV Pricing

MUV is billed per piece at the S.S. Price. Distributor and Retail prices are the recommended onward billing rates (+7% at each level). MRP is inclusive of GST.

04

Order Units

Seera, Yuva, Shine Plus, Aatmanirbhar and Bartan Tub are billed per full Box / Bag as per the case configuration shown. MUV is billed per piece.

05

Free-Goods Scheme

Every Box of Seera / Yuva detergent cake carries 3 pcs of ₹10 MRP detergent powder free. Detergent powder Bags carry the scheme shown against them. Free goods are supplied over and above the ordered quantity, carry no invoice value and may be revised or withdrawn by the Company with notice.

06

MRP Compliance

Products must not be sold above the printed MRP at any level of the distribution chain.

07

Price Revisions

The Company reserves the right to revise prices, schemes and pack configurations. Orders are billed at the rate in force on the date of invoicing.

08

Confidentiality

This document is issued exclusively to the authorised Super Stockist named below and is not to be shared with distributors, retailers or third parties.
