# Portal Separation Audit

Founder/Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Executive, Distributor, and Super Stockist experiences have distinct navigation, dashboards, terminology, permission sets, and data scope. They share business services and tables but not a generic role-hidden portal workflow.

Sales Executive cannot fulfil distributor orders. Distributor cannot fulfil S.S. orders. S.S. cannot access Admin. Delivery User is limited to delivery execution. Distributor and S.S. records require explicit active party membership.
