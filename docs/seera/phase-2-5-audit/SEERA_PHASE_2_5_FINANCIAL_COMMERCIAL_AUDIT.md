# Financial and Commercial Integrity Audit

- Price and credit terms are effective-dated and append-only.
- Orders store SKU, pack, MRP, price, tax, scheme, partner, and credit facts at transaction time.
- Company-to-S.S. credit is prohibited; verified advance is required before confirmation.
- S.S.-to-Distributor credit is configurable by distributor, with warning, hold, block, and approval override decisions.
- Contractual due, grace, promise, and payment concepts remain separate.
- Booked orders never create delivered-sales achievement.
- Payment proof submission is not payment verification.
- Phase 8 remains authoritative for accounting reconciliation and financial settlement.
