# Founder Intelligence Knowledge Factory — Knowledge Objects

32 Knowledge Objects across 10 Engines: 29 reasoning frameworks (Founder Note / Derived Founder
Principle), 2 Gap Records, 1 closing Repository Governance Summary. Every reasoning KO applies
the Founder Thinking Pipeline (`ENGINE_MASTER.md` §2) and addresses the Founder Decision Model
(`ENGINE_MASTER.md` §3) — fields not distinctive to a given KO are answered by the general model
and not repeated verbatim, per Token Efficiency Mode.

# Engine 1 — Founder Philosophy Engine™

## KO-FD-PH-001 — The Founder Direction Framework

- **Purpose:** Capture the reasoning cycle the Founder applies when developing or judging any
  identity, creative, or foundational direction.
- **Scope:** Search → Question → Reject or Qualify → Clarify → Preserve → Continue.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part I, Ch.1 (Founder Direction & Origin); Constitution
  Articles 1, 2, 8.
- **Outputs:** A six-stage reasoning framework applicable to any "is this direction right"
  question, not only brand identity.
- **Dependencies:** None (Engine-opening KO).
- **Relationships:** feeds KO-FD-PH-002; grounds Constitution Article 2 (Premium Is Coherence).
- **Governance Rules:** *"Preserve only what strengthens the whole"* — not what has effort
  invested in it.
- **Validation Rules:** All six framework stages preserved in exact order; the Weak-Practice-vs-
  Muv-Standard table preserved as the framework's evaluative test.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-08-01 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Founder Note

**Content — Understand/Care:** Applied whenever a direction (identity, creative concept, or
foundational choice) needs judgment, not just approval. **Reason (the framework itself):** 1.
**Search** — what is the strongest direction available? 2. **Question** — does it truly feel
like Muv? 3. **Reject or qualify** — what is weak, incomplete, or unresolved? 4. **Clarify** —
what exactly must change or remain fixed? 5. **Preserve** — what has now been approved? 6.
**Continue** — what is the next correct step? **Preferred Decision:** a direction is not judged
by how much effort it cost, only by whether it strengthens the whole once judged in real
application (*"Judge proposals in their real application"*). **When NOT to use:** this framework
governs foundational/identity-level judgment, not routine execution decisions already covered by
an approved direction — re-litigating settled identity choices via this framework wastes the
"Preserve" stage's purpose. **Review Trigger:** a new direction proposal that has not yet passed
stage 3 (Reject or qualify).

## KO-FD-PH-002 — The Keep Muving Loop as the Operating Reasoning Cycle

- **Purpose:** Capture the Founder's default execution rhythm — the cycle every unit of work,
  not only identity work, should move through.
- **Scope:** See Clearly → Clean → Decide → Move → Verify → Continue; the Movement-Is-Not-Hurry
  distinction.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part I, Ch.2 (Keep Muving); Constitution Article 1.
- **Outputs:** A six-stage operating cycle; a five-row Weak-Urgency-vs-Discipline table.
- **Dependencies:** KO-FD-PH-001.
- **Relationships:** feeds KO-FD-PH-003; directly grounds the Founder Thinking Pipeline's own
  Understand/Verify/Learn stages (`ENGINE_MASTER.md` §2).
- **Governance Rules:** *"Keep Muving does not mean moving fast while the work remains wrong."*
- **Validation Rules:** All six loop stages preserved in order; the five-row table preserved.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-08-01 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Founder Note

**Content — the loop:** 1. **See Clearly** — is the work correct, is something missed, is the
direction still aligned? 2. **Clean** — remove confusion, weak execution, forgotten decisions,
avoidable friction. 3. **Decide** — identify the next step, not everything at once. 4. **Move** —
execute; do not let planning become permanent delay. 5. **Verify and remember** — check the
result, record the decision. 6. **Continue** — the end of one step is the beginning of the next.
**Trade-offs:** speed vs. correctness is a false choice under this framework — the loop is
designed to deliver *quick, correct* progress, not fast-but-wrong progress, by making
verification a mandatory stage rather than an optional afterthought. **Risks:** skipping "See
Clearly" or "Verify" produces "scattered motion" that looks like progress but repeats mistakes.
**Review Trigger:** any work unit that has moved through "Decide" and "Move" without a
corresponding "Verify" record.

## KO-FD-PH-003 — The Darshan Test (Strategic Decision Filter)

- **Purpose:** Capture the five-part strategic filter the Founder applies to judge whether an
  initiative belongs at Muv, distinct from tactical/technical correctness.
- **Scope:** Movement, Trust, Memory, Execution, Identity.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part I, Ch.3 (MUV Darshan); Constitution Article 10.
- **Outputs:** A five-question strategic filter; the From-Darshan-to-Decision flowchart (Purpose
  → Fit → Status → Trade-offs → Ownership → Execution → Memory).
- **Dependencies:** KO-FD-PH-002.
- **Relationships:** feeds KO-FD-PH-004; grounds Constitution Article 10 (Trust Compounds Over
  Generations); relates to KO-FD-OP-001/002 (Optimization Engine) where growth tactics must also
  pass this filter.
- **Governance Rules:** These questions *"do not replace technical standards. They provide a
  strategic filter."*
- **Validation Rules:** All five test questions and all seven flowchart stages preserved.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-08-01 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Founder Note

**Content — the Darshan Test:** **Movement** — does this remove an obstacle or help someone
continue? **Trust** — can Muv keep the promise consistently? **Memory** — will this strengthen a
coherent, recognisable brand? **Execution** — can the company deliver it properly? **Identity**
— is it recognisably Muv rather than a generic market idea? **Objective:** an initiative that
passes technical review can still fail this test if it doesn't build trust or memory over the
long horizon — *"chosen for generations"* changes the time horizon of every evaluation.
**Preferred Decision:** apply the Darshan Test *before* technical feasibility review, since a
strategically-misaligned initiative that is technically excellent is still the wrong initiative.
**When NOT to use:** routine execution of an already-approved initiative does not need to re-pass
this test — it governs whether to start something, not how to execute something already started.

# Engine 2 — Founder Decision Intelligence™

## KO-FD-DI-001 — The Decision Ladder

- **Purpose:** Capture the five-state model that distinguishes an idea's actual authority level
  at any point in time.
- **Scope:** Proposal → Exploration → Qualified Direction → Approved Decision → Preserved Rule.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part I, Ch.5 (Founder Position, Revision & Decision Record);
  Constitution Articles 5, 6.
- **Outputs:** Five-state ladder with permitted use per state.
- **Dependencies:** None (Engine-opening KO).
- **Relationships:** feeds KO-FD-DI-002/003/004; grounds Constitution Articles 5 and 6 directly;
  is the direct model behind the Founder Decision Model's own "Preferred Decision" field
  (`ENGINE_MASTER.md` §3).
- **Governance Rules:** *"Many failures occur between approval and preservation. A decision can
  be made correctly and still disappear."*
- **Validation Rules:** All five states and their permitted-use column preserved together, never
  collapsed.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-08-01 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Founder Note

**Content:**

| State | Meaning | Permitted Use |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal | An option has been suggested | Evaluate; do not present as policy |
| Exploration | Alternatives are being compared | Test and document |
| Qualified direction | Part is accepted; details remain open | Apply only the settled portion |
| Approved decision | The Founder has clearly chosen | Implement within stated scope |
| Preserved rule | Approval is recorded for future teams | Use as controlling direction |

**Reasoning:** most operational failures in this whole knowledge-ecosystem's own working history
occur when a Proposal or Exploration-stage idea is treated with Approved-Decision authority. **When
NOT to use:** do not apply this ladder to a fact already established as a Preserved Rule
elsewhere (e.g., a frozen Marketing Knowledge Factory domain) — re-litigating a Preserved Rule
via this ladder without a new Founder Decision violates Constitution Article 13.

# Engine 3 — Founder Product Intelligence™

## KO-FD-PI-001 — Commercial/Knowledge Separation Reasoning

- **Purpose:** Capture the reasoning pattern behind separating volatile facts (that change
  without notice) from stable intelligence (that a knowledge system should own).
- **Scope:** Why MRP/price/stock/images are excluded from the Product Knowledge Factory; the
  general principle behind that exclusion.
- **Inputs:** Product Knowledge Factory `CONSTITUTION.md` Articles 2-4, `FOUNDER_RULES.md`
  `FR-001`; Constitution (this repository) Article 3.
- **Outputs:** A general reasoning test for "does this fact belong in a knowledge system, or must
  it always be read live."
- **Dependencies:** None (Engine-opening KO).
- **Relationships:** authoritative reference to Product Knowledge Factory `CONSTITUTION.md`
  Article 2 (frozen); feeds KO-FD-PI-002.
- **Governance Rules:** *"A Knowledge Factory that stores a commercial value will silently go
  stale the first time that value changes... reading commercial fields live, every time,
  structurally eliminates this failure mode."*
- **Validation Rules:** The distinguishing test (does the value change independently of the
  knowledge system's own editorial process?) preserved as the reasoning core, not just the
  eleven-field example list.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-08-01 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Founder Note

**Content — the reasoning test:** a fact belongs in a knowledge system if changing it requires
the knowledge system's own editorial judgment (a formulation change, a new safety finding, a
revised SOP). A fact must be read live, never stored, if it changes on its own system of record
(a CMS, a database) **without** the knowledge system being told — because nothing in a knowledge
system's own validation discipline can catch staleness it has no mechanism to detect. **When NOT
to use:** this test does not apply to facts that are genuinely static once approved (formulation,
safety classification) — applying it there would wrongly turn stable knowledge into a live-lookup
dependency it doesn't need.

- **Purpose:** Capture the escalated version of the Never-Invent rule (Constitution Article 3)

- **Inputs:** Product Knowledge Factory `FOUNDER_RULES.md` `FR-005`; Constitution (this
  repository) Article 3.
- **Outputs:** The six-field mandatory-documentation-or-explicit-gap pattern; the three named
  forbidden claim categories (dermatological, antibacterial, skin-safe).
- **Dependencies:** KO-FD-PI-001.
- **Relationships:** authoritative reference to Product Knowledge Factory `FR-005` (frozen);
  relates to Marketing Knowledge Factory's own Claims Control discipline (`KO-CC-CH2-003`).
- **Governance Rules:** *"Never invent dermatological claims. Never invent antibacterial claims.
  Never invent skin-safe claims."*
- **Validation Rules:** Silence is never acceptable for a designated-critical field — the
  absence itself must be recorded, not omitted.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-08-01 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Founder Note

**Content — the pattern:** when a product or claim category is classified as safety-critical, a
fixed set of fields becomes mandatory *documentation targets*, not optional content — each must
be answered with a real sourced fact or an explicit `Unknown — Founder Decision Required` marker.
**Reasoning:** this generalizes beyond product safety — any reasoning domain with a
high-consequence failure mode (a specific claim category identified as risky after a real near-
miss, e.g. Body Wash's zero-safety-content finding) earns the right to name explicit forbidden
categories, once, after the risk is discovered — this is Constitution Article 8 (Formalize After
Proof) applied to risk governance specifically.

## KO-FD-PI-003 — Formalize-Precedent-Once-Proven Reasoning

- **Purpose:** Capture the general pattern for when an ad hoc working method should become a
  permanent rule.
- **Scope:** The Variant Inheritance / Knowledge Reuse precedent-to-rule pattern.
- **Inputs:** Product Knowledge Factory `FOUNDER_RULES.md` `FR-003`, `FR-004`; Constitution (this
  repository) Article 8.
- **Outputs:** A three-condition test for "should this ad hoc pattern become a standing rule."
- **Dependencies:** KO-FD-PI-002.
- **Relationships:** authoritative reference to `FR-003`/`FR-004` (frozen); this is the direct
  elaboration of Constitution Article 8.
- **Governance Rules:** A pattern earns rule status by recurring successfully, not by being
  designed well in the abstract.
- **Validation Rules:** The three conditions preserved as a set, never reduced to "it worked
  once."
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-08-01 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Derived Founder Principle

**Content — the three conditions, derived from `FR-003`/`FR-004`'s shared shape:** 1. the
pattern was applied at least once, ad hoc, under real constraints (not a hypothetical design). 2.
it produced a real, disclosed benefit or avoided a real, disclosed failure (Floor Cleaner's
variant-inheritance architecture; three prior packages' informal reuse-report practice). 3. the
Founder explicitly elevated it — formalization is never self-authorized by the pattern's own
apparent success. **When NOT to use:** a pattern that has only been proposed, never actually run,
does not qualify — that is Decision Ladder "Exploration" (`KO-FD-DI-001`), not "Preserved Rule."

- **Version:** 1.0
