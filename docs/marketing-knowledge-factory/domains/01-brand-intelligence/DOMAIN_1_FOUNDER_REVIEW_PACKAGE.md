## 3. Real findings surfaced (not invented, not hidden)

- **Cross-source conflict, resolved by existing authority:** `PHASE_3_BRAND_EXPERIENCE_
  LANGUAGE.md` forbids the word "Premium"; the Knowledge Library treats it as an approved,
  load-bearing pillar. Resolved by citing the pre-existing resolution in `PHASE_1A_KNOWLEDGE_
  REFERENCES.md` (Knowledge Library supersedes) — never re-decided by this domain.
- **Documentation gap, found and later resolved by direct Founder ruling:** "Muving Soon™" is
  used in production and named in the Muv AI Sutra, but was absent from the Knowledge Library's
  own Correct-Use criteria for Keep Muving™. Flagged as `Founder Decision Required`
  (`KO-BI-CH3-005`) rather than resolved by inference — the Founder subsequently issued
  **`FR-008`**, approving it as a campaign teaser, restricted from ever replacing "Keep Muving™."
- **Retroactive grounding, correctly labeled as backward:** Chapter 5's formal "Identity Decision
  States" taxonomy (Proposed/Exploratory/Qualified/Approved/Deferred/Rejected/Superseded) defines
  the word "exploratory" that Chapters 1 and 4 had already used informally, for the "affordable
  luxury from India" positioning claim and the category-colour list respectively. This is a real
  fact about the source's own chapter ordering, not an authoring inconsistency.
- **One item remains genuinely open:** "affordable luxury from India" (`KO-BI-CH1-005`) is
  formally **Exploratory**, not Approved — still `Founder Decision Required` if a firmer claim is
  ever wanted.
