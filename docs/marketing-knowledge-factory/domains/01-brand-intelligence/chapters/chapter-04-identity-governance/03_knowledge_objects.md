## KO-BI-CH4-001 — Typography System

- **Name:** Typography System
- **Purpose:** Give any AI/design-adjacent system the governing typography principles — and
  honestly communicate that no final corporate typeface is yet established.
- **Scope:** §12 Typography System, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part III, Chapter 14, §12.
- **Outputs:** Six Permanent Typography Principles; the Text Role table; the Decision Point.
- **Dependencies:** KO-BI-CH1-001.
- **Relationships:** "Premium character" (Principle 2) governed by `KO-BI-CH1-006`, referenced.
- **Governance Rules:** A named font becomes permanent only after formal approval, licensing
  confirmation, and cross-format testing — never assumed settled from a single use.
- **Validation Rules:** Must not invent or imply a named typeface exists — the source is
  genuinely silent on this, and this KO preserves that silence rather than filling it.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified (this section is honestly thin in the source itself — not
  under-researched here, genuinely minimal there)

**Content:** The Founder repeatedly requested better fonts, clearer text, refined typography, and
consistent casing. **A final named corporate typeface family is not established in the
Foundation evidence.**

**Permanent Typography Principles:** (1) Clarity before decoration. (2) Premium character without
reduced readability. (3) Consistent hierarchy. (4) Correct Muv and Keep Muving casing. (5)
Sufficient size and spacing for the medium. (6) No uncontrolled font substitution.

| Text Role | Required Character |
|---|---|
| Brand headline | Distinctive, refined, highly legible |
| Section heading | Clear hierarchy and confidence |
| Body copy | Comfortable reading across print and digital |
| Product information | Accurate, compact, and legible |
| Legal or mandatory text | Readable at final production size |
| Call to action | Clear and immediately recognisable |

**Decision Point:** A named font becomes permanent only after formal approval, licensing
confirmation, and testing across packaging, digital, marketplace, and document use.

## KO-BI-CH4-002 — Colour Philosophy

- **Purpose:** Give the governing colour-layer rules and the correctly-scoped category-colour
  exploration (settled vs. exploratory).
- **Scope:** §13 Colour Philosophy, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part III, Chapter 14, §13.
- **Outputs:** The five-layer Color Governance table; the category-colour exploration list, with

- **Dependencies:** KO-BI-CH1-005 (Positioning — colour supports "premium character," directly
  named in the opening line).
- **Relationships:** None new beyond dependency.
- **Governance Rules:** *"Do not create permanent colour values from screenshots or generated
  images. Use approved master specifications once formally locked."* — a direct, load-bearing
  constraint on any future Creative Intelligence domain.
- **Validation Rules:** The category-colour list must never be presented as final/settled — the
  source's own caveat ("evidence-supported... but not a complete final colour specification")
  must always accompany it.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified (Color Governance table) / the category-colour list
  itself carries the source's own **exploratory, not-yet-final** status — preserved, not upgraded

**Content:** Colour should support recognition, category navigation, emotion, and premium
character without fragmenting the brand.

**Founder exploration (exploratory, not final):** lavender or purple directions for body care;
pink directions for skin care; emerald green for home care; carbon-gloss orange for car care.
These are evidence-supported category directions, but not a complete final colour specification.

| Layer | Rule |
|---|---|
| Core brand colour | Requires controlled master definition |
| Category colour | Supports navigation without replacing the Muv identity |
| Product colour | Must reflect approved product and packaging truth |
| Background colour | Must preserve logo and text clarity |
| Accent colour | Should guide attention, not dominate |

Do not create permanent colour values from screenshots or generated images. Use approved master
specifications once formally locked.

## KO-BI-CH4-004 — Photography & Image Style

- **Purpose:** Give the governing photography principles and the load-bearing Protection Rule,
  fulfilling the forward relationship predicted in Chapter 2.
- **Scope:** §15 Photography & Image Style, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part III, Chapter 14, §15.
- **Outputs:** Seven Photography Principles; the Image Type treatment table; the Protection Rule.
- **Dependencies:** KO-BI-CH2-003 (Truthful Application principle).
- **Relationships:** **Fulfills the forward relationship predicted in Chapter 2**
  (`KO-BI-CH2-003` → Chapter 4's Photography). This Protection Rule and Chapter 2's "Truthful
  Application" are complementary, near-identical statements of the same underlying constraint —
  cited as reinforcing, not duplicating, each other.
- **Governance Rules:** *"Never 'improve' a product visual by changing the real bottle, label,
  colour, size, or approved design."* — directly binding on any future Creative/Content
  Intelligence domain generating product imagery.
- **Validation Rules:** All seven principles and the full Image Type table must be preserved
  together — never cited partially in a way that drops the Protection Rule.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** The Founder repeatedly asked for realistic, high-quality visuals and instructed that
products must not be altered.

**Photography Principles:** (1) Product form must remain accurate. (2) Labels must remain
unchanged unless revision is explicitly authorized. (3) Product size and height must not be
distorted. (4) Images should feel premium and realistic. (5) The product should remain the
visual truth. (6) Emotional context may support the product but must not fabricate history or
performance. (7) Background and props should fit the category and customer situation.

| Image Type | Required Treatment |
|---|---|
| Product hero | Clear product, accurate label, controlled premium setting |
| Benefit image | Benefit must be approved and visually understandable |
| Lifestyle image | Realistic context without false product behaviour |
| Before/after | Use only when the comparison is truthful and authorized |
| Brand story | Do not introduce unrelated products or invented history |
| Video | Maintain identity consistency across the full sequence |

**Protection Rule:** Never "improve" a product visual by changing the real bottle, label,
colour, size, or approved design.

## KO-BI-CH4-005 — Packaging Identity Principles

- **Purpose:** Give the identity-continuity rules for packaging, while correctly deferring
  detailed production to its own, out-of-scope volume.
- **Scope:** §16 Packaging Identity Principles, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part III, Chapter 14, §16.
- **Outputs:** The packaging requirement list; the Fixed-vs-Variable elements table.
- **Dependencies:** KO-BI-CH2-003 (Truthful Application), KO-BI-CH4-004 (shares the same
  never-alter-the-real-product discipline).
- **Relationships:** Explicitly out-of-scope pointer: detailed packaging *production* belongs to
  Volume IV — MUV Design System™ (Part IV), a different domain entirely, never duplicated here.
- **Governance Rules:** Category variation must remain subordinate to the fixed/protected
  elements — never let a campaign sticker or seasonal panel override the approved Muv mark or
  mandatory information.
- **Validation Rules:** The Fixed-vs-Variable table's two columns must never be merged or
  presented as equally changeable.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** Detailed packaging production belongs to Volume IV — MUV Design System™. The brand
layer controls identity continuity only.

**Packaging must:** use the approved logo; preserve correct name and tagline treatment; keep
category variation subordinate; maintain product and mandatory information accuracy; avoid
unauthorized label change; remain recognisable across sizes and variants.

| Fixed or Protected | Controlled Variable |
|---|---|
| Approved Muv mark | Category accent |
| Product identity | Variant or fragrance distinction |
| Mandatory information | Supporting imagery |
| Approved label structure | Campaign sticker or secondary panel, if authorized |
| Trademark treatment | Format-specific scaling within approved rules |

- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

new approved master; (3) distinguish editable digital assets from printed stock; (4) replace
without altering unrelated content; (5) verify every output; (6) record what remains in
transition; (7) prevent the old logo from returning through templates or stored assets.

## KO-BI-CH4-009 — Chapter Governance Summary

- **Purpose:** Preserve the chapter's closing governance content, matching the established
  pattern from Chapters 1–3.
- **Scope:** Common Mistakes, Best Practices, Action Checklist, Chapter Summary.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part III, Chapter 14, closing subsections.
- **Outputs:** A consolidated do/don't reference, a 9-item Action Checklist, and the chapter's
  own transition line.
- **Dependencies:** KO-BI-CH4-001 through 008.
- **Relationships:** Mirrors the established Chapter-Governance-Summary pattern exactly.
- **Governance Rules:** No new rule invented.
- **Validation Rules:** All 9 checklist items in original order; must not add an item not present
  in source.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**Common Mistakes:** Creating permanent font or colour rules from generated output. Using
category colour as a substitute for the main identity. Mixing icon styles. Altering a product
while creating a "better" image. Updating one digital page while leaving old identity elsewhere.
Producing marketplace assets without checking packaging truth.

**Best Practices:** Maintain controlled master assets. Separate fixed identity from category
expression. Test typography and colour in final-size conditions. Use reusable digital and
marketplace templates. Audit old identity across every channel after a change.

**Action Checklist:**
- [ ] Confirm master logo and current version.
- [ ] Check typography hierarchy and casing.
- [ ] Use only approved colour specifications.
- [ ] Verify icon consistency.
- [ ] Confirm image realism and product accuracy.
- [ ] Protect packaging elements.
- [ ] Check digital components across devices.
- [ ] Review the full marketplace image set.
- [ ] Record transitional old-identity occurrences.

**Chapter Summary:** *"Identity Governance converts brand intention into repeatable control.
Typography, colour, graphics, photography, packaging, digital, and marketplace expression may
vary by context, but they must remain subordinate to one protected Muv identity."* Source's own
transition line: *"Next: Brand Decision History establishes how identity changes are approved,
preserved, and protected"* — the sourced justification for Chapter 5 following next.
