## KO-BI-CH3-001 — Pronunciation Standard

- **Name:** Pronunciation Standard
- **Purpose:** Give any AI/voice/audio system the single correct pronunciation and the discipline
  for reinforcing it.
- **Scope:** Primary Pronunciation, Pronunciation Reinforcement, and the Identity Rule callout.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part III, Chapter 13 (Pronunciation Standard section).
- **Outputs:** The pronunciation rule, the reinforcement channel list, the identity-protection
  constraint.
- **Dependencies:** KO-BI-CH1-001 (Brand Identity Foundation).
- **Relationships:** Paired with KO-BI-CH3-002 (Written Identity — nested under the same source
  heading).
- **Governance Rules:** Identity Rule (verbatim): *"Never solve pronunciation by inventing a new
  spelling or adding punctuation to the brand without Founder approval."*
- **Validation Rules:** The problem statement ("M-U-V" letter-reading) and the "move" answer must
  both be present — neither alone is sufficient context.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** The Founder identified a recurring problem: people read the letters separately as
"M-U-V," while the intended pronunciation is **move**. **Primary Pronunciation: "Muv is
pronounced 'move.'"** Reinforcement is achieved through repetition across real touchpoints:
spoken video and audio; staff and partner training; customer-service introductions; Keep Muving
messaging; pronunciation guidance in internal and franchise material; consistent written casing.

**Identity Rule:** Never solve pronunciation by inventing a new spelling or adding punctuation to
the brand without Founder approval.

**Content:** Use **Muv** in customer-facing text where the approved brand form requires a capital
M and lowercase uv. The formal titles of the Knowledge Library and registered systems may
continue to use approved uppercase styling such as MUV Darshan™.

## KO-BI-CH3-003 — Tone of Voice

- **Purpose:** Give any AI content-generation system the exact tone-of-voice table and its
  Founder-communication context.
- **Scope:** §7 Tone of Voice, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part III, Chapter 13, §7.
- **Outputs:** Six-row tone table; the Founder-Led Directness distinction between internal and
  customer-facing register.
- **Dependencies:** KO-BI-CH2-002 (Brand Personality — tone directly expresses personality).
- **Relationships:** "Premium" row governed by `KO-BI-CH1-006` (referenced, not re-recorded).
- **Governance Rules:** Customer-facing language must preserve clarity without copying internal
  urgency/frustration — a real, load-bearing distinction between how the Founder communicates
  internally and how any AI must communicate externally.
- **Validation Rules:** All six rows, three columns, exact match required.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** *"The Muv tone should reflect its personality."*

| Tone Quality | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Clear | Direct sentences and understandable actions | Technical or decorative confusion |
| Warm | Respectful, caring customer language | Forced intimacy |
| Confident | Decisive approved statements | Unsupported certainty |
| Progressive | Language of useful movement | Empty motivational slogans |
| Premium | Controlled rhythm and refined wording | Pretentious vocabulary |
| Practical | Explain what the customer should know or do | Generic filler |

**Founder-Led Directness:** The Founder's communication is often direct — weak work is named as
weak, missing verification is identified, the next step is requested clearly. Customer-facing
language should preserve clarity without copying frustration or internal urgency; internal teams
should be direct about quality while remaining professional and specific.

## KO-BI-CH3-004 — Messaging System

- **Purpose:** Give any AI content-generation or content-review system the correct message
  hierarchy and, critically, which authority governs each message type.
- **Scope:** §8 Messaging System, complete — Figure 3.1, Levels 1–4, Authority table.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part III, Chapter 13, §8.
- **Outputs:** Four-level hierarchy; six-row Authority table.
- **Dependencies:** KO-BI-CH1-003 (Mission — Level 1 message derives from it).
- **Relationships:** Directly relevant to future Domain 4 (Campaign Intelligence) and Domain 6
  (Content Intelligence) — the Authority table is the single most important governance artifact
  for any future content-generation domain, since it dictates which system a given claim type
  must be checked against before publishing.
- **Governance Rules:** No message may be generated at a level without deferring to its named
  authority (e.g., a price/offer claim always requires Commercial authority, never generated from
  brand-identity knowledge alone).
- **Validation Rules:** All six Authority-table rows must be preserved with their exact authority
  name; never abbreviate "MUV Darshan™" to "philosophy" or similar.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**Figure 3.1 — Muv Messaging Hierarchy:** Brand meaning → customer relevance → proof or approved
product truth → action.

- **Level 1 — Master Brand Message:** *"Muv supports cleaner, more cared-for living so people can
  Keep Muving."*
- **Level 2 — Category Message:** Explain the approved role of the category (home care, fabric
  care, body care, car care, or another approved area).
- **Level 3 — Product Message:** Use verified product benefits, directions, size, fragrance,
  ingredients, or performance information controlled by the owning product system.
- **Level 4 — Action:** Invite a relevant next step (explore, understand, purchase, enquire, use
  correctly, or continue).

| Message Type | Required Authority |
|---|---|
| Founder philosophy | MUV Darshan™ |
| Brand identity message | MUV Brand Sutra™ |
| Product claim | Product authority |
| Price or offer | Commercial authority |
| Campaign line | Marketing authority |
| Customer-service promise | Operating authority |

## KO-BI-CH3-005 — Keep Muving™ Philosophy

- **Purpose:** Give the complete, correct usage rules for the brand's primary movement
  expression, including its relationship to the legacy "Magic in Muv" phrase, and honestly flag
  the one real gap this domain's own research surfaced.
- **Scope:** §9 Keep Muving™ Philosophy, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part III, Chapter 13, §9; Muv AI Sutra §6.6 (cited only for
  the gap note, not as a Knowledge Library substitute).
- **Outputs:** Correct Use (5 items), Incorrect Use (5 items), the Magic-in-Muv relationship rule,
  and an explicit, unresolved gap flag.
- **Dependencies:** KO-BI-CH1-001 (the "Magic in Muv" → "Keep Muving" evolution first noted
  there).
- **Relationships:** Fulfills the forward relationship predicted in Chapter 1
  (`KO-BI-CH1-001` → Chapter 3's Keep Muving™ Philosophy).
- **Governance Rules:** Per the source's own Decision Point: when an asset contains "Magic in
  Muv," confirm whether it is a protected existing label element, an active campaign expression,
  or historical language before changing it — never assume by default. **Per Founder Decision
  `FR-008` (2026-07-31): "Muving Soon™" is approved as an official campaign teaser for product
  launch teasers, new fragrance launches, new category launches, website feature announcements,
  and marketing countdown campaigns — restricted: it must never replace "Keep Muving™," which
  remains the permanent MUV brand philosophy.**
- **Validation Rules:** Any use of "Muving Soon™" must cite `FR-008` as its authority. It must
  never be substituted for, or presented as equivalent to, "Keep Muving™" in any context.
- **Version:** 1.1
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created. 2026-07-31 — Founder Decision `FR-008` recorded:
  "Muving Soon™" gap resolved (approved as campaign teaser, restricted from replacing "Keep
  Muving™"); Evidence Classification updated from Founder Decision Required to Founder Approved.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified (Correct/Incorrect Use, Magic-in-Muv rule) / **Founder
  Approved** (the "Muving Soon™" gap — resolved by `FR-008`, see below; no longer open)

**Content:**

Keep Muving is the primary movement expression of the brand. Volume I defines its philosophy;
this section governs its use in identity and messaging.

**Correct Use:** Use it where movement, completion, progress, or continued care is relevant.
Preserve the spelling **Muving**. Maintain approved casing. Use trademark treatment where
formally approved. Let it conclude or reinforce a meaningful experience.

**Incorrect Use:** Repeating it on every page or image. Using it to hide poor customer
experience. Applying it to unrelated claims. Changing the spelling to match generic grammar.
Presenting it as proof of product performance.

**Relationship with "Magic in Muv":** "Magic in Muv" formed part of the earlier product and logo
direction. Later decisions gave Keep Muving stronger brand-wide importance. Existing approved
label use of "Magic in Muv" should not be removed casually; new use must follow the current
identity decision.

**Decision Point:** When an asset contains "Magic in Muv," confirm whether it is a protected
existing label element, an active campaign expression, or historical language before changing it.

**✅ Gap resolved — Founder Decision `FR-008` (2026-07-31):** "Muving Soon™" was flagged as a gap
because it is used in production (the live homepage) and is explicitly named in the Muv AI Sutra
(§6.6, Marketing AI's Brand Language Protection list, as approved teaser language), yet did not
appear anywhere in this chapter's Correct Use / Incorrect Use lists or the Knowledge Library's
searchable text generally — a coverage gap, not a conflict, and one this Knowledge Object
deliberately did not resolve by inference at authoring time.

**The Founder has since ruled directly:** "Muving Soon™" is **approved as an official campaign
teaser**, for: product launch teasers; new fragrance launches; new category launches; website
feature announcements; marketing countdown campaigns. **Restriction:** it must **never replace
"Keep Muving™"** — Keep Muving™ remains the permanent MUV brand philosophy; Muving Soon™ is a
narrower, campaign-scoped teaser expression, not a substitute for or variant of the philosophy
itself. This ruling is now binding and supersedes the prior "open" status — but the original gap
finding is preserved above (not deleted) as the audit trail for how the resolution was reached.

## KO-BI-CH3-007 — Chapter Governance Summary

- **Purpose:** Preserve the chapter's closing governance content, matching `KO-BI-CH1-007`/
  `KO-BI-CH2-007`'s established role.
- **Scope:** Common Mistakes, Best Practices, Chapter Summary.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part III, Chapter 13, closing subsections.
- **Outputs:** A consolidated do/don't reference plus the chapter's own transition line.
- **Dependencies:** KO-BI-CH3-001 through 006.
- **Relationships:** Mirrors the established Chapter-Governance-Summary pattern exactly.
- **Governance Rules:** No new rule invented.
- **Validation Rules:** Must not add an item not present in source.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**Common Mistakes:** Pronouncing Muv as separate letters. Using uppercase and lowercase
inconsistently. Writing generic luxury language without customer meaning. Turning Keep Muving
into motivational filler. Mixing brand philosophy with unverified product claims.

**Best Practices:** Train pronunciation verbally, not only through documents. Use message
hierarchy before drafting copy. Keep one primary message per asset. Write for clarity before
cleverness. Check every claim with its owning system.

**Chapter Summary:** *"Muv should sound as controlled as it looks. Pronunciation, casing, tone,
message hierarchy, and Keep Muving usage must work together to create a recognisable and
trustworthy voice."* Source's own transition line: *"Next: Identity Governance controls
typography, colour, imagery, packaging, and channel consistency"* — the real, sourced
justification for Chapter 4 following next in this domain's frozen sequence.
