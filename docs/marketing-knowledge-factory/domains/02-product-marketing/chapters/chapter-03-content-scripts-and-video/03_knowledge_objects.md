## KO-PM-CH3-001 — Content Philosophy

- **Purpose:** Define content's role and forbid the two named failure modes (noise, unsupported
  risk).
- **Scope:** Content Philosophy, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part VII, Ch.33 (Content Philosophy).
- **Outputs:** Figure 3.1; the volume-vs-truth warning.
- **Dependencies:** KO-PM-CH2-001 (Campaign Philosophy).
- **Relationships:** Governs every later KO in this chapter.
- **Governance Rules:** *"Content volume without customer value creates noise. High production
  quality without product truth creates risk."*
- **Validation Rules:** Figure 3.1's 7-stage sequence preserved in order.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** Content is the repeatable expression of Muv value across the customer journey — it
should help a person notice, understand, evaluate, use, trust, repeat, or share.

**Figure 3.1 — Content Value System:** Customer question → Approved answer → Best content format
→ Consistent production → Distribution → Response → Learning.

Content volume without customer value creates noise. High production quality without product
truth creates risk.

## KO-PM-CH3-012 — AI-Assisted Marketing & AI Content Control

- **Purpose:** Give the exact, complete boundary of permitted vs. forbidden AI action in
  marketing content production — the single most self-referentially important Knowledge Object
  in this repository to date.
- **Scope:** AI-Assisted Marketing, AI Content Control (Figure 3.5), complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part VII, Ch.33 (AI-Assisted Marketing, AI Content
  Control).
- **Outputs:** 10 permitted AI functions; 6 forbidden AI actions; the 8-stage AI Marketing Review
  Gate.
- **Dependencies:** `KO-BI-CH5-004` (Message Protection, Domain 1), KO-PM-CH2-008 (Claim
  Discipline), KO-PM-CH2-004 (Storytelling's never-invent list).
- **Relationships:** This KO governs how every other Knowledge Object in this entire Marketing
  Knowledge Factory should itself be *used* by a downstream AI system — a genuinely reflexive
  relationship, noted explicitly rather than left implicit.
- **Governance Rules:** *"AI must not: invent Founder history; alter product, label, or approved
  identity; invent claims, reviews, results, or customers; publish without human review; convert
  public competitor content into Muv truth; hide uncertainty or source state."* Every AI-drafted
  asset must pass all 8 stages of the Review Gate before publication — no stage may be skipped.
- **Validation Rules:** Both lists (permitted, forbidden) must be reproduced in full and never
  merged or abbreviated; the Review Gate's 8 stages must be preserved in exact order, ending in
  human approval.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**AI may assist with:** idea expansion; brief structuring; draft variants; format adaptation;
transcription; research organisation; asset prompts; content calendars; consistency checks;
performance analysis.

**AI must not:** invent Founder history; alter product, label, or approved identity; invent
claims, reviews, results, or customers; publish without human review; convert public competitor
content into Muv truth; hide uncertainty or source state.

**Figure 3.5 — AI Marketing Review Gate:** Approved inputs → Generated draft → Product-truth
review → Brand review → Claim review → Visual/rights review → Channel review → Human approval.

## KO-PM-CH3-014 — Chapter Governance Summary

- **Purpose:** Preserve the chapter's closing governance content.
- **Scope:** Common Mistakes, Best Practices, Action Checklist, Key Takeaways, Chapter Summary.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part VII, Ch.33, closing subsections.
- **Outputs:** Consolidated do/don't reference; 10-item Action Checklist; 4 Key Takeaways;
  Chapter Summary.
- **Dependencies:** KO-PM-CH3-001 through 013.
- **Relationships:** Mirrors the established Chapter-Governance-Summary pattern.
- **Governance Rules:** No new rule invented.
- **Validation Rules:** All 10 checklist items in original order.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**Common Mistakes:** Posting without a customer or business objective. Treating content
frequency as strategy. Writing scripts around unsupported transformations. Using AI-generated
product inaccuracies. Sending advertisement traffic to a weak landing experience. Measuring
performance only by platform metrics. Repurposing until qualification or context disappears.

**Best Practices:** Maintain a controlled message and proof library. Build content around
customer questions. Produce in systems, not isolated emergencies. Review product and identity at
full resolution. Connect media to the complete D2C journey. Archive high-performing learning, not
only files.

**Action Checklist:**
- [ ] Content objective and journey stage defined.
- [ ] Customer question is real and specific.
- [ ] Message and proof are approved.
- [ ] Brief identifies format, channel, and protected elements.
- [ ] Script preserves product and claim truth.
- [ ] Website/landing experience supports the message.
- [ ] Search intent and social role are clear.
- [ ] Email/WhatsApp use is permission-aware.
- [ ] AI output passed human review.
- [ ] Measurement includes customer quality and learning.

**Key Takeaways:** Content should help the customer progress, not merely fill a calendar. Every
script and digital asset begins with approved inputs. Digital growth depends on the full
journey, not media alone. AI expands production capacity only when truth and approval controls
remain human-governed.
