## KO-PM-CH4-001 — Reach Philosophy & Creator Role

- **Purpose:** Define reach correctly (access, not proof of value) and the limited role creators
  play relative to brand/product authority.
- **Scope:** Reach Philosophy, Creator Role.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part VII, Ch.34 (lines 7572–7593).
- **Outputs:** Figure 4.1; six creator functions; the authority-boundary statement.
- **Dependencies:** KO-PM-CH1-006 (Customer Journey — Figure 4.1 mirrors that journey's early
  stages).
- **Relationships:** Governs every later KO in this chapter.
- **Governance Rules:** *"They do not own product truth or brand authority."* — the single most
  important constraint on any creator relationship.
- **Validation Rules:** Figure 4.1's six-stage sequence preserved in order.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** Reach is access to attention. It is not proof of relevance, trust, purchase
quality, or long-term value.

**Figure 4.1 — Reach to Relationship:** Relevant exposure → Credible understanding → Informed
action → Product experience → Continued value → Advocacy. Marketing should optimise the whole
path, not only the first number.

**Creators may help Muv:** reach a relevant community; demonstrate use; translate a product into
everyday context; create culturally or locally relevant communication; provide authentic
questions and feedback; strengthen launch visibility. **They do not own product truth or brand
authority.**

## KO-PM-CH4-004 — Authenticity and Control

- **Purpose:** Give the exact boundary model resolving the tension between creator authenticity
  and brand/claim control.
- **Scope:** Authenticity and Control, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part VII, Ch.34 (Authenticity and Control).
- **Outputs:** The locked-vs-free boundary model.
- **Dependencies:** KO-PM-CH4-003.
- **Relationships:** Directly extends `KO-BI-CH5-004` (Message Protection) and
  `KO-PM-CH2-008` (Claim Discipline) into the creator-content context specifically.
- **Governance Rules:** *"Lock product facts, claims, mandatory disclosure, and protected
  identity; allow appropriate creator expression inside that boundary; review before publication
  when required."*

  distinct steps, never collapsed.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.

**Content:** Creator content should sound natural to the creator while remaining accurate.
Over-scripted content can weaken trust; uncontrolled content can create false claims. The
solution is a clear boundary: lock product facts, claims, mandatory disclosure, and protected
identity; allow appropriate creator expression inside that boundary; review before publication
when required.

## KO-PM-CH4-006 — Reviews and Reputation & Reputation Response

- **Purpose:** Give the honest-review-handling rules and the seven-stage response system.
- **Scope:** Reviews and Reputation, Reputation Response (Figure 4.2).
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part VII, Ch.34 (Reviews and Reputation, Reputation
  Response).
- **Outputs:** Seven review-handling rules; Figure 4.2; seven issue-classification categories.
- **Dependencies:** KO-PM-CH1-006 (Customer Feedback as Learning).
- **Relationships:** Directly extends `KO-BI-CH5-004` Message Protection into review-response
  content specifically.
- **Governance Rules:** *"Never fabricate, purchase, suppress, or selectively misrepresent
  reviews."* — the strongest single anti-fabrication statement in this domain to date.
  *"The owning function handles the resolution; marketing protects clarity and tone"* — a real
  scope boundary (marketing doesn't resolve product/service complaints itself).
- **Validation Rules:** All seven review-handling rules and all seven Figure 4.2 stages
  preserved.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** Reviews are customer evidence and public trust signals. Marketing should: invite
honest feedback without coercion; monitor questions and patterns; respond respectfully; avoid
defensive claims; route product/service issues; **never fabricate, purchase, suppress, or
selectively misrepresent reviews**; use testimonial content only with appropriate authenticity,
context, and permission.

**Figure 4.2 — Review Response System:** Observe → Verify identity/context → Classify → Respond
→ Resolve/escalate → Learn → Close loop.

**Differentiate:** product complaint; delivery/channel issue; misunderstanding; preference;
misuse; false or abusive content; safety concern. The owning function handles the resolution;
marketing protects clarity and tone.

## KO-PM-CH4-012 — Chapter Governance Summary

- **Purpose:** Preserve the chapter's closing governance content.
- **Scope:** Common Mistakes, Best Practices, Action Checklist, Key Takeaways, Chapter Summary.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part VII, Ch.34, closing subsections.
- **Outputs:** Consolidated do/don't reference; 10-item Action Checklist; 4 Key Takeaways;
  Chapter Summary.
- **Dependencies:** KO-PM-CH4-001 through 011.
- **Relationships:** Mirrors the established pattern.
- **Governance Rules:** No new rule invented.
- **Validation Rules:** All 10 checklist items in original order.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**Common Mistakes:** Choosing reach over relevance. Giving creators unclear claim boundaries.
Buying content without defining usage rights. Treating reviews only as reputation risk. Calling
repeated promotions a community. Passing unqualified leads to sales. Expanding geographically
before supply and service readiness.

**Best Practices:** Select creators through audience and trust evidence. Use clear briefs with
controlled flexibility. Treat reviews as both public communication and learning. Build retention
before scaling acquisition. Give sales current, audience-specific assets. Test expansion in a
bounded market.

**Action Checklist:**
- [ ] Reach objective and audience defined.
- [ ] Creator relevance and risk assessed.
- [ ] Brief, claims, disclosure, approval, and rights controlled.
- [ ] Pricing basis and deliverables documented.
- [ ] Reviews monitored and routed honestly.
- [ ] Community provides real member value.
- [ ] Acquisition quality extends beyond click cost.
- [ ] Retention communication is useful and permission-aware.
- [ ] Sales assets are current and traceable.
- [ ] Market expansion includes supply, service, and learning readiness.

**Key Takeaways:** Reach becomes valuable only when it creates relevant understanding and
experience. Creators extend communication, not product or brand authority. Reputation and
reviews must be handled honestly. Sustainable expansion connects marketing with supply, sales,
service, and local learning.

**Chapter Summary:** *"Creators, Influencers & Reach establishes how Muv uses external voices and
channel communication to build relevant access without surrendering truth, trust, or
governance."* Source's transition line: *"Next: Marketing Decisions & Learning governs metrics,
experiments, economics, documentation, and future marketing leadership"* — the sourced
justification for Chapter 5 following next.
