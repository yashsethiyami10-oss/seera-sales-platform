## 1. Source-mapping finding — disclosed prominently, not buried

The same full-Library research pass performed for Domain 7 (see
`domains/07-growth-and-optimization-intelligence/DOMAIN_MASTER.md` §1) applies here. **No Part
or chapter is dedicated to "Marketing Operations & Learning."** The operational/governance/
decision-recording half of Domain 2's Chapter 5 (Marketing Decisions & Learning) — Decision
Integrity, the Marketing Decision Record format, Rejected Approaches, Marketing Documentation,
Asset Version Control, Marketing Governance, AI Governance, Future Marketing Leadership, and the
Marketing Operating Rhythm — is the entirety of the relevant content, and it is already owned in
full by Domain 2 (frozen).

Part XI ("People, Leadership, Learning & Culture," Chapters 51-57) was specifically checked
given "Learning" appears in both its Part title and this domain's name. It was ruled out: its
Chapter 53 ("Learning & Capability Development") is HR/employee-training content (talent
acquisition, onboarding, career development, leadership development, organizational health) —
not marketing-decision learning. Citing it here would misrepresent an HR chapter as marketing
content; it is correctly left uncited and out of scope for this Marketing Knowledge Factory
domain.

**Conclusion, stated plainly:** Domain 8, like Domain 7, contains **zero newly-transcribed
source content**. Its Knowledge Objects are either citation-only references to Domain 2's
already-frozen operational/governance content, or a Gap Record for a named Founder Original IP
capability confirmed absent from the source.

## 2. What this domain actually is

The **operational counterpart to Domain 7**: where Domain 7 collects Domain 2's
measurement/economics/experimentation content under a growth frame, Domain 8 collects Domain
2's decision-recording/documentation/governance content under an operations-and-learning frame.
Both draw from the same source chapter (Part VII Ch.35, Domain 2's own Chapter 5) because that
chapter's own Purpose statement literally names both halves: *"Define marketing measurement,
acquisition economics, return on marketing investment, brand health, campaign evaluation,
experimentation, decision records, documentation, governance, and future marketing
leadership."* The first six items are Domain 7's; the last four (plus Decision Integrity and
Rejected Approaches from earlier in the chapter) are Domain 8's. This split is disclosed, not
arbitrary — it follows the chapter's own two-part internal structure.

## 3. Founder Original IP — Gap Record only, never designed

Per Founder Execution Protocol v4.0, **Founder Decision Engine™** is the one named Founder
Original IP capability scoped to marketing operations and decision-making. Confirmed absent by
direct search. Recorded as `KO-MO-007`, never designed. See Domain 7's `DOMAIN_MASTER.md` §3
for the full nine-engine cross-reference table (six creative-scoped engines already recorded in
Domain 6; two growth-scoped engines recorded in Domain 7; this one operations-scoped engine
recorded here).
