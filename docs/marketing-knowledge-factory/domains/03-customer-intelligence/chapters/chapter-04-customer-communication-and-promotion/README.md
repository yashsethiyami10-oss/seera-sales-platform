## Disclosed title/content divergence

This chapter's title says **"& Promotion"**, but its real body content — confirmed by a
dedicated verbatim re-extraction — is **entirely complaint-handling, recovery, feedback, review
analysis, and trust restoration**. None of its subsection headings relate to promotional or
campaign content. This divergence, first flagged during Domain 3's initial research phase, is
disclosed here rather than silently resolved by inventing promotional content the source does
not contain, or by silently renaming the chapter.
