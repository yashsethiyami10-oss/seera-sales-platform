## KO-CI-CH7-001 — Business Intelligence Discipline

- **Purpose:** Preserve the eight-field discipline a useful intelligence product must state,
  and the warning against decision-less dashboards.
- **Scope:** §4.6 Business Intelligence, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part XII, Ch.61, §4.6 (lines 16153–16168).
- **Outputs:** Eight-field intelligence-product discipline; the "visibility theatre" warning.
- **Dependencies:** KO-CI-CH1-001.
- **Relationships:** governs KO-CI-CH7-002.
- **Governance Rules:** *"Dashboards that accumulate numbers without decisions create
  visibility theatre."*
- **Validation Rules:** All eight fields preserved together; the warning never dropped.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** Business intelligence should connect measures to decisions.

**A useful intelligence product states:** the question; the definition of each measure; the
period and scope; the source; known limitations; comparison or context; responsible owner; and
the decision or action it informs.

Dashboards that accumulate numbers without decisions create visibility theatre.

## KO-CI-CH7-002 — Customer Insights: Evidence Sources & the Observation-vs-Explanation Rule

- **Purpose:** Preserve the eight possible customer-insight evidence sources and the
  observation-is-not-explanation discipline — this chapter's most important governance rule.
- **Scope:** §4.7 Customer Insights, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part XII, Ch.61, §4.7 (lines 16170–16183).
- **Outputs:** Eight evidence sources; the observation-vs-explanation rule.
- **Dependencies:** KO-CI-CH7-001.
- **Relationships:** relates to (complements, does not duplicate) KO-CI-CH4-005 (Feedback
  Collection) and KO-CI-CH4-006 (Review Analysis & Customer Insight) — this is the
  data-governance/BI view of largely the same evidence Chapter 4 covers from the CX-operations
  view.
- **Governance Rules:** *"Observation is not explanation. Muv must distinguish what happened
  from why it happened."*
- **Validation Rules:** All eight evidence sources preserved together; the observation/
  explanation rule never softened or dropped.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** Customer insight may combine: purchase and journey evidence; search and content
behaviour; support interactions; reviews and feedback; returns and complaints; research;
channel patterns; and product-use learning where appropriately obtained.

Observation is not explanation. Muv must distinguish what happened from why it happened.
