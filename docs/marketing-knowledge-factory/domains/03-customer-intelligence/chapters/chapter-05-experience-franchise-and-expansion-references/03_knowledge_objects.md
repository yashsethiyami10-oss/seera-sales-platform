**Figure 5.1 — Relationship Development:** Useful promise → Reliable experience → Fair recovery

correct use; convenience; habit; service; trust; replenishment timing; relevant communication.
Analyse repeat by product, channel, cohort, and customer context. Do not interpret all
non-repeat as rejection without evidence.

- **Purpose:** Give the six-stage measurement framework, the seven-layer measurement table, the
  ten potential CX metrics, and the ten-field measurement-discipline definition.
- **Scope:** Experience Measurement (Figure 5.3), CX Metrics, Measurement Discipline, complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part IX, Ch.45 (Experience Measurement, CX Metrics,

- **Governance Rules:** *"No metric alone defines experience quality."* *"Do not compare
  numbers collected through materially different methods as if they were identical."*
- **Validation Rules:** Figure 5.3's six stages, the seven-row table, all ten metrics, and all
  ten discipline fields preserved; both rules never dropped.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content — Figure 5.3 — Experience Measurement Framework:** Promise → Journey measure →
Operational evidence → Customer perception → Outcome → Learning.

| Layer | Examples |
|---|---|
| Accessibility | Can customers find information and support? |
| Effort | How difficult is selection, purchase, use, or resolution? |
| Reliability | Order accuracy, delivery, product consistency, response completion |
| Perception | Satisfaction, trust, clarity, confidence |
| Behaviour | Repeat, retention, advocacy, churn |
| Recovery | Resolution, repeat contact, reopened cases, recurrence |
| Learning | Root causes, actions, effectiveness |

**CX Metrics — potential metrics require defined methods:** satisfaction after a specific
interaction; customer effort; recommendation/advocacy measure; repeat or retention;
first-contact or end-to-end resolution; complaint and severity rate; response and resolution
time; contact rate by journey; review themes; trust/clarity research. No metric alone defines
experience quality.

**Measurement Discipline — for each metric define:** question; scope; customer; event/timing;
method; sample; calculation; owner; limitation; decision use. Do not compare numbers collected
through materially different methods as if they were identical.

**Content — MUV Customer Experience in One View:**
