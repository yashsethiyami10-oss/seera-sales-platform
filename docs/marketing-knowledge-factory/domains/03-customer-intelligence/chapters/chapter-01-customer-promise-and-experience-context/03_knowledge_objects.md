## KO-CI-CH1-005 — Customer Journey Map & Journey Mapping

- **Purpose:** Give the company-wide, ten-stage journey map and the required documentation
  fields for any real journey map — distinct from Domain 2's marketing-specific journey map.
- **Scope:** Experience beyond Transaction (Figure 1.2), Journey Mapping.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part IX, Ch.41 (Experience beyond Transaction, Journey

- **Outputs:** Figure 1.2, ten-row table; twelve documentation fields.
- **Dependencies:** KO-CI-CH1-001.
- **Relationships:** Related to, but genuinely distinct from, `KO-PM-CH1-006` (Customer Journey
  Map, Domain 2 — 8 stages: Unaware→Advocating, marketing-task framing). This KO's 10 stages
  (Awareness→Advocacy) are framed as company-wide *responsibility*, not marketing task — cited
  against Domain 2's version explicitly, never merged into one "canonical" journey map, since
  the source itself maintains them as two distinct artifacts for two distinct purposes.
- **Governance Rules:** *"Journey maps must reflect actual channels and behaviour, not an
  idealised diagram."*
- **Validation Rules:** All ten stages and twelve documentation fields preserved.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** The experience begins before purchase and continues after product use.

**Figure 1.2 — Muv Customer Journey Map:**

| Stage | Customer question | Muv responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Is this relevant to me? | Communicate a real problem and value |
| Discovery | What is Muv and what does it offer? | Make identity and range understandable |
| Evaluation | Is this right and trustworthy? | Provide accurate product, price, proof, use, and support |
| Purchase | Can I buy confidently and easily? | Ensure clear choice, terms, payment, and availability |
| Delivery | Will the right product arrive correctly? | Fulfil accurately, safely, and transparently |
| First use | How do I use it and what should I expect? | Provide guidance and reachable help |
| Ongoing use | Does it remain valuable and consistent? | Deliver product truth and learning support |
| Repeat | Is it worth choosing again? | Reinforce value, availability, and trust |
| Loyalty | Does Muv understand and respect me? | Build continuity without pressure |
| Advocacy | Should I recommend Muv? | Enable authentic sharing and protect reputation |

**Journey Mapping — for each journey, document:** customer type; objective; stages; touchpoints;
customer questions; expected experience; owner; evidence/system dependency; friction; failure
and escalation; measurement; improvement. Journey maps must reflect actual channels and
behaviour, not an idealised diagram.

standard or system changes? How will improvement be validated?

## KO-CI-CH1-009 — Chapter Governance Summary

- **Purpose:** Preserve the chapter's closing governance content.
- **Scope:** Common Mistakes, Best Practices, Action Checklist, Key Takeaways, Chapter Summary.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part IX, Ch.41, closing subsections.
- **Outputs:** Consolidated do/don't reference; 9-item Action Checklist; 4 Key Takeaways; Chapter
  Summary.
- **Dependencies:** KO-CI-CH1-001 through 008.
- **Relationships:** Establishes this domain's own Chapter-Governance-Summary pattern, reused
  from Domains 1–2.
- **Governance Rules:** No new rule invented.
- **Validation Rules:** All 9 checklist items in original order.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**Common Mistakes:** Calling support the complete customer experience. Designing journeys from
internal departments. Treating a completed transaction as a successful relationship. Promising
response levels without operational capacity. Solving one complaint without finding repeated
friction. Measuring only satisfaction after the fact.

**Best Practices:** Map real journeys by customer and channel. Give every moment of truth an
owner. Review handoffs. Combine customer voice with operational evidence. Design for normal use
and failure recovery. Preserve learning across teams.

**Action Checklist:**
- [ ] Customer and journey objective defined.
- [ ] Promise and expected reality aligned.
- [ ] All stages and touchpoints mapped.
- [ ] Moments of truth identified.
- [ ] Owners and handoffs explicit.
- [ ] Standards and escalation paths approved.
- [ ] Customer effort and dignity considered.
- [ ] Evidence and measurement plan defined.
- [ ] Improvement decision and owner recorded.

**Key Takeaways:** Customer experience is the company-wide result of promise and execution.
Trust grows through consistent truth, respect, and recovery. The journey extends from awareness
through advocacy. Handoffs must be invisible in effort but visible in ownership.
