## KO-CI-CH6-001 — CRM as Commercial Memory & the Fourteen Maintained Fields

- **Purpose:** Establish CRM's role as the commercial memory of relationships and commitments,
  and preserve the fourteen fields a CRM record must maintain.
- **Scope:** CRM System, opening definition and field list, complete (this is the entire
  imported excerpt's substantive content).
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part VIII, Ch.40, "CRM System" subsection (lines
  9700–9721).
- **Outputs:** CRM definition statement; fourteen maintained fields.
- **Dependencies:** KO-CI-CH1-001, KO-CI-CH1-005.
- **Relationships:** governs KO-CI-CH6-002; relates to (complements, does not duplicate)
  KO-CI-CH1-005 (Customer Journey Map) and KO-CI-CH3-005 (Query Handling & Support Channels) —
  CRM is the commercial-relationship record; those chapters are the experience/service-
  operations views of the same customer.
- **Governance Rules:** None new in this KO — a definitional/structural reference.
- **Validation Rules:** All fourteen fields preserved in original order; the definition
  sentence never dropped.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** CRM is the commercial memory of relationships and commitments.

**Maintain:** account/contact identity; role and consent/preference; source; need and
qualification; territory/channel; interactions; opportunity stage/value; products and terms;
objections; samples/proposals; next action; orders, fulfilment, collection; complaint/service
links; owner and history.
