## 1. The source-mapping question this domain had to resolve first

Unlike Domains 1 and 2, which each mirrored one clean source Part (Part III, Part VII), no
single Part in the MUV Knowledge Library cleanly matches "Customer Intelligence":

- The literal phrase "customer intelligence" appears nowhere in the 81-chapter Library.
- "Customer segmentation," "customer data platform," and "customer profile" never appear
  anywhere.
- CRM is owned entirely by Part VIII (Sales & Distribution System™), Chapter 40.
- Part IX (MUV Customer Experience™), the only Part literally about "customer," was verified at
  chapter-body level to be about service delivery, support operations, complaint recovery, and
  loyalty/retention — not customer data or behavioral intelligence.
- Part XII Ch.61 has one small "Customer Insights" subsection (§4.7) among ten data-governance
  sections.

Per Source Discipline's explicit instruction ("record it, trace it, escalate through Founder
Decision Required — never silently resolve uncertainty"), this was escalated to the Founder via
a direct question rather than guessed. **The Founder's decision: "Synthesized domain: Part IX +
CRM + Data Insights."** This made Domain 3 a real, disclosed 7-chapter departure from Domains
1-2's clean 5-chapter-per-Part pattern — recorded in full in `domains/03-customer-intelligence/
README.md` and never silently normalized to look like a standard mirror.

- **Domains 1 and 2 (both frozen) were never modified.** All 6 cross-domain KOID citations
  (1 to Domain 1, 5 to Domain 2) independently PowerShell-verified to resolve to real, existing,
  unaltered content in each domain's live registry.

  independent Explore-agent verbatim re-extraction of its own source text.

  subsections (CRM System; §4.6-4.7) of their much larger host chapters — confirmed by
  reporting, in each chapter's own requirement analysis, exactly what comes immediately before
  and after the imported text, and by the small, proportionate KO counts rather than padding to
  match Chapters 1-5's size.

## 4. Real findings surfaced (not invented, not hidden)

- **Chapter 4's title says "& Promotion," but its actual body is entirely complaint-handling,
  recovery, feedback, and trust restoration** — confirmed by two independent verbatim
  re-extractions. No promotional content was invented to fill the apparent gap; the divergence
  is disclosed in the chapter's own README and requirement analysis.
- **Chapter 2's Food-Service Experience Reference ("Mewadi" section)** borrows analogical CX
  evidence from a distinct food-service and franchise business. Its governing disclaimer —
  *"These references do not create unsupported Muv product rules or duplicate Mewadi business
  ownership"* — is preserved verbatim in `KO-CI-CH2-002` every time that content is cited, and
  this domain never asserts the borrowed lessons as fact about MUV's own operations.
- **Part IX has its own closing Part Summary** ("MUV Customer Experience in One View," 13-row
  table + 10 Master Principles + 14-item Master Checklist), confirmed via re-verification and
  preserved as a dedicated, explicitly-flagged KO (`KO-CI-CH5-012`) — this became the real
  grounding source for the Knowledge Coverage Audit's Part A.
- **Multiple explicit anti-fabrication rules were preserved verbatim across the domain**,
  including: *"This volume does not invent universal compensation"* (Ch.4); *"Never invent
  reviews, testimonials, community size, or satisfaction"* (Ch.5); *"Observation is not
  explanation. Muv must distinguish what happened from why it happened"* (Ch.7).
- **One safety-priority governance rule** required special preservation: *"Never delay a safety
  or serious product-quality escalation while attempting to satisfy a normal response target"*
  (Ch.3's Escalation Process WARNING) — never softened or paraphrased.
