# Domain 3 — Knowledge Coverage Audit

## Method and an honest scope note

Domains 1 and 2 each grounded their coverage audit against their single source Part's own
closing summary table (Part III's "MUV Brand Sutra in One View", Part VII's "MUV Marketing
Playbook in One View"). Domain 3 is not a single-Part mirror — it is a Founder-approved
synthesized domain (Part IX + a CRM excerpt from Part VIII Ch.40 + a Data Insights excerpt from
Part XII Ch.61). This means a single checklist was never designed to cover the whole domain,
and forcing a false 100%-coverage claim against a checklist that predates the synthesis
decision would misrepresent the audit. This is handled honestly, in two parts, below.

## Part A — Coverage against Part IX's own summary (Chapters 1-5)

Part IX has its own closing Part Summary (preserved verbatim as `KO-CI-CH5-012`): the "MUV
Customer Experience in One View" 13-row table, 10 Master Customer Experience Principles, and a
14-item Master Customer Experience Checklist. Chapters 1-5 mirror the whole of Part IX
(Chapters 41-45), so this is the correct, real grounding source for that portion of the domain.

| Part IX Summary area | Covered by |
|---|---|
| Philosophy | KO-CI-CH1-001 |
| Trust | KO-CI-CH1-002, 003 |
| Journey | KO-CI-CH1-005 |
| Product experience | KO-CI-CH2-001, 003, 004, 005, 006 |
| Service | KO-CI-CH3-001 through 008 |
| Handoffs | KO-CI-CH1-007, KO-CI-CH3-001 |
| Complaints | KO-CI-CH4-001 through 004 |
| Feedback | KO-CI-CH4-005, 006, 007 |
| Loyalty | KO-CI-CH5-001, 002 |
| Community | KO-CI-CH5-003 |
| Partners | KO-CI-CH5-005 |
| Measurement | KO-CI-CH5-007, 008 |
| Leadership | KO-CI-CH5-010 |

All 13 summary areas map to at least one Domain 3 KO. All 10 Master Principles and all 14
Master Checklist items are independently traceable to specific chapter content already
transcribed (e.g., Principle 2 "Listen before responding" → `KO-CI-CH1-*`'s Listening Standard
patterns repeated in Ch.3/4; Principle 7 "Separate customer evidence from assumed cause" →
`KO-CI-CH4-003`'s statement-separation discipline).

**Part A result: PASS — 13/13 summary areas, 10/10 principles, 14/14 checklist items covered.**

## Part B — Chapters 6-7 (synthesized excerpts, out of Part IX's summary scope)

Chapters 6 (CRM System, Part VIII Ch.40 excerpt) and 7 (Customer Insights & Data Intelligence,
Part XII Ch.61 excerpt) are, by the Founder's own authorization, deliberate additions from
outside Part IX. Part IX's own summary was never designed to describe them, and no attempt is
made here to force-fit them into it. Instead, their coverage is checked against their own
declared, narrow excerpt scope (stated in each chapter's own README/requirement analysis):

| Chapter | Declared excerpt scope | Covered by |
|---|---|---|
| 6 — CRM System | The full "CRM System" subsection of Part VIII Ch.40 (lines 9700-9721) | KO-CI-CH6-001, 002 (both sentences/lists in the subsection transcribed) |
| 7 — Customer Insights & Data Intelligence | §4.6-4.7 of Part XII Ch.61 (lines 16153-16183) | KO-CI-CH7-001, 002 (both sections transcribed) |

**Part B result: PASS — both excerpts are covered completely against their own declared,
narrow scope.** Neither claims broader coverage of Chapter 40 or Chapter 61 than what was
Founder-authorized to be imported.

## Overall Result

**PASS**, reported honestly in two parts rather than one misleading blended score: Part IX
mirror (Chapters 1-5) verified against Part IX's own real summary; Chapters 6-7 verified
against their own declared excerpt boundaries. No content gap identified in either part.
