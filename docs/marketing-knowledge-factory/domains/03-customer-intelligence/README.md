# Domain 3 — Customer Intelligence

> Domain-level Requirement Analysis. Executed as a **complete implementation batch** per the
> Founder's directive. This domain required a **Founder escalation before any chapter was
> authored** — recorded here in full, since it materially shapes this domain's structure.

---

## Domain scope

Customer Intelligence, as named by the Founder, is the knowledge domain covering who the
customer is, how MUV understands and serves them across the full experience, and how customer
relationship/behavioral data is governed.

## Source ambiguity — escalated, not silently resolved

Before authoring, a real source-mapping question was investigated and found to have **no clean
answer** — unlike Domain 1 (Part III) and Domain 2 (Part VII), no single Part in the MUV
Knowledge Library is a direct match for "Customer Intelligence":

- The literal phrase **"customer intelligence" appears nowhere** in the 81-chapter library.
- **"Customer segmentation," "customer data platform," "customer profile"** never appear
  anywhere either.
- **CRM** (the closest analog to "who the customer is / relationship memory") is owned entirely
  by **Part VIII — MUV Sales & Distribution System™**, Chapter 40.
- **Part IX — MUV Customer Experience™** (the only Part literally about "customer") was verified,
  chapter body by chapter body, to be about service delivery, support operations, complaint
  recovery, and loyalty/retention — i.e., *how MUV treats customers during/after interaction* —
  not customer data, behavior, or segmentation. Its own front matter explicitly hands campaign
  strategy to Part VII and commercial transactions/channels to Part VIII.
- **Part XII — MUV Technology & Digital Ecosystem™**, Chapter 61 ("Data Architecture &
  Intelligence System") contains one small "Customer Insights" subsection (§4.7) embedded among
  ten data-governance sections — not a customer-intelligence chapter itself.

This finding was reported to the Founder as an escalation (per Source Discipline: "record it,
trace it, escalate through Founder Decision Required — never silently resolve uncertainty")
rather than guessed at.

## Founder Decision (resolving the ambiguity)

**The Founder selected a synthesized domain**: Domain 3 is built primarily from Part IX, with
the CRM System section of Part VIII Chapter 40 and the Customer Insights/Data section of Part
XII Chapter 61 §4.6–4.7 pulled in as two additional, explicitly-sourced chapters. This is a real
departure from the clean one-domain-equals-one-Part pattern established in Domains 1–2 — recorded
here transparently, not smoothed over as if it were the same kind of mapping.

## Governance boundary (per Part IX's own repeated cross-ownership statement)

*"Volume VII — MUV Marketing Playbook™ owns campaign strategy. Volume VIII — MUV Sales &
Distribution System™ owns commercial transactions and channels. Volume V — MUV Product Sutra™
owns product truth. Volume VI — MUV Manufacturing System™ owns manufacturing and quality
execution. Volume X — MUV Business Blueprint™ owns franchise architecture and company-wide
compliance."* Domain 3 never absorbs any of this — cited, never restated. Domains 1 (frozen) and
2 (frozen) are read-only — referenced, never modified.
