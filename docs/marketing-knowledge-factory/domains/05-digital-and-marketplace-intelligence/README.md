## Source mapping — a two-Part synthesis

A dedicated research pass (mirroring Domain 3's own discipline) found no single Part cleanly
covers "Digital & Marketplace Intelligence." The real content is split across two Parts, at two
different altitudes:

- **Part II — "Enterprise Operating System, Website Architecture & Knowledge Governance"**
  (source foundation: Volume II — MUV Operating System™), Chapters 6–10 — the **implementation/
  engineering layer**: how Muv's owned digital platform is structured, connected, secured, and
  evolved.
- **Part XII — "Technology, Digital Ecosystem, Data & AI"** (source foundation: Volume XII —
  MUV Technology & Digital Ecosystem™), Chapters 58–64 — the **strategic/governance layer**:
  the digital ecosystem, the website's customer-facing vision, data architecture, AI systems,
  technology operations, and future digital evolution.

Both Parts discuss "website architecture," but Part II is "how to build and validate it" while
Part XII Chapter 60 is "what the Digital Flagship must achieve and how it's governed" — a real,
disclosed altitude difference, not duplication. Both are imported in full (except where
Domain 3 already claims a piece — see below), following the same "mirror the source faithfully"
discipline used for Domain 3's Part IX portion.

## Critical cross-domain duplication risks — identified and resolved before authoring began

**Risk 1 — Data Architecture & Intelligence System (Part XII, Chapter 61).** Domain 3 (frozen)
already imported two of this chapter's ten sections — §4.6 (Business Intelligence) and §4.7
(Customer Insights) — as `KO-CI-CH7-001` and `KO-CI-CH7-002`. This domain's own Chapter 9
(mapping to the same Ch.61) imports **only** the eight remaining sections (§4.1–4.5, §4.8–4.10)
and explicitly cites the two Domain 3 KOs at the point in the chapter sequence where §4.6–4.7
would appear, rather than re-transcribing them — the same citation-only-KO pattern Domain 4's
Chapter 5 used for the CRM System subsection.

**Risk 2 — Marketplace content (Amazon/Flipkart, listing/conversion).** Domain 4 (frozen)
already fully covers marketplace *commercial and operational* content (Part VIII Chapters
38–39: account governance, catalogue, inventory, orders, settlements, listing philosophy,
conversion diagnostics). This domain's own content — the D2C/marketplace *digital-experience*
framing in Part XII Ch.59 §2.4 ("D2C and Marketplace Relationship") and Ch.60's
checkout/cart/order-flow mechanics — is a genuinely different altitude (digital-experience
architecture vs. commercial/operational execution), confirmed by direct textual comparison, and
is recorded as `complementsNotDuplicates` wherever the overlap is real, never silently merged.

**Risk 3 — Digital marketing execution (Part VII, Domain 2).** Part XII only references
marketing as a system category at architecture level (e.g., "the marketing system" as one
ecosystem component); it does not contain marketing execution content. No overlap found beyond
a Volume-name-level architectural mention, cited not absorbed.
