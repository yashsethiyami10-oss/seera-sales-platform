| Premium Expression | Operating Constraint |
|---|---|
| Cinematic hero | Must not hide the primary action |
| Motion | Must not reduce clarity or performance |
| Refined typography | Must remain readable |

## KO-DM-CH2-010 — Chapter Governance Summary

- **Purpose:** Preserve the chapter's closing governance content.
- **Scope:** Common Mistakes, Best Practices, Action Checklist, Chapter Summary.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part II, Ch.7, closing subsections.
- **Outputs:** Consolidated do/don't reference; 7-item Action Checklist; Chapter Summary.
- **Dependencies:** KO-DM-CH2-001 through 009.
- **Relationships:** Mirrors the established pattern.
- **Governance Rules:** No new rule invented.
- **Validation Rules:** All 7 checklist items in original order.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**Common Mistakes:** Designing a beautiful homepage without complete customer journeys. Mixing
consumer purchase and business enquiry into one unclear form. Using mock data as though it were
live. Copying another brand's identity while borrowing a useful structural pattern. Checking
one viewport and declaring the site responsive.

**Best Practices:** Begin with a complete site and journey map. Give each page one clear
primary responsibility. Use real data as soon as the interface depends on it. Keep premium
presentation subordinate to clarity and truth. Test customer and business journeys separately.

**Action Checklist:**
- [ ] Confirm homepage purpose and primary action.
- [ ] Map categories and products.
- [ ] Define consumer, business, and franchise journeys.
- [ ] Verify every form's destination.
- [ ] Check content ownership.
- [ ] Review mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- [ ] Confirm that every visible capability is real or clearly identified as future.

**Chapter Summary:** *"Website Architecture organises the customer's movement from discovery to
action. It combines premium presentation with truthful content, clear journeys, responsive
behaviour, and controlled editing."* Source's own transition line: *"Next: Backend, Integration
& Automation connects that experience to real operations"* — the sourced justification for
Chapter 3 following next.
