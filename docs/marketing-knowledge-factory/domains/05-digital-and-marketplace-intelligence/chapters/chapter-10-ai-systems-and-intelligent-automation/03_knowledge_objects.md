## KO-DM-CH10-001 — MUV AI Vision

- **Purpose:** Establish AI as an assistive capability, never a source of founder authority or
  invisible decision ownership.
- **Scope:** MUV AI Vision (Figure 5.1), complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part XII, Ch.62 (MUV AI Vision).
- **Outputs:** Eight assistive uses; Figure 5.1 seven-element architecture formula.
- **Dependencies:** None (chapter-opening KO).
- **Relationships:** governs KO-DM-CH10-002 through 010.
- **Governance Rules:** *"AI is an assistive capability, not a source of founder authority,
  factual certainty, or invisible decision ownership."*
- **Validation Rules:** All eight uses and Figure 5.1's seven elements preserved.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** Muv may use AI to extend organizational capability by helping people: find and
organize information; analyze evidence; prepare drafts; support routine workflows; improve
access to approved knowledge; identify patterns and exceptions; assist customers within
governed boundaries; and accelerate learning and execution. AI is an assistive capability, not
a source of founder authority, factual certainty, or invisible decision ownership.

Service + Human Review + Logged Outcome + Learning.

## KO-DM-CH10-006 — Automation Opportunity Framework

- **Purpose:** Preserve the eight opportunity-strength factors and the six automation-avoidance
  conditions.
- **Scope:** Automation Opportunity Framework (Figure 5.2), complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part XII, Ch.62 (Automation Opportunity Framework).
- **Outputs:** Eight strength factors; six avoidance conditions.
- **Dependencies:** KO-DM-CH10-005.
- **Relationships:** feeds KO-DM-CH10-007.
- **Governance Rules:** None new beyond the avoidance conditions themselves.
- **Validation Rules:** All eight factors and six conditions preserved.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content — an automation opportunity is strongest when the work is:** frequent; rule-based;
stable enough to define; measurable; supported by reliable data; costly or error-prone when
manual; reversible or safely testable; and able to retain exception handling.

**Avoid automating work when:** the process is not understood; rules change constantly;
consequences are high and controls are weak; inputs are unreliable; human empathy or judgment
is central; or ownership is unclear.

## KO-DM-CH10-012 — Chapter Governance Summary

- **Purpose:** Preserve the chapter's closing governance content.
- **Scope:** Best Practices, Action Checklist, Chapter Summary.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part XII, Ch.62, closing subsections.
- **Outputs:** Best-practices reference; 9-item Action Checklist; Chapter Summary.
- **Dependencies:** KO-DM-CH10-001 through 011.
- **Relationships:** Mirrors the established pattern.
- **Governance Rules:** No new rule invented.
- **Validation Rules:** All 9 checklist items in original order.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**Best Practices:** Start with low-risk, measurable tasks. Require source references where
factual accuracy matters. Keep draft and approved output separate. Test failure modes, not only
ideal prompts. Teach users both capability and limitation. Monitor actual outcomes after
release. Revalidate when models, data, prompts, or workflows change.

**Action Checklist:**
- [ ] Define the AI use case and status.
- [ ] Assign business and technical ownership.
- [ ] Review data, confidentiality, privacy, and vendor treatment.
- [ ] Identify foreseeable errors and consequences.
- [ ] Design meaningful human review.
- [ ] Test representative and adverse cases.
- [ ] Define monitoring, escalation, and shutdown.
- [ ] Document approval, changes, and limitations.
- [ ] Confirm no unsupported AI capability is presented as current.

**Chapter Summary:** *"Muv's AI system must combine approved knowledge, governed data, defined
tasks, appropriate technology, meaningful human review, and documented learning. AI may
accelerate work and improve access, but it cannot become founder authority, unquestioned truth,
or hidden decision ownership."* No explicit "Next:" line exists; Chapter 11 (Technology
Operations & Security) follows as Part XII's own next chapter.
