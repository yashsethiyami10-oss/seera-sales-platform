**Content — Muv Technology Operating System (complete operating model):**

|---|---|

| Digital ecosystem | Connect platforms, channels, operations, partners, and data |
| Website and customer technology | Deliver the governed MUV Digital Flagship™ experience |

**Master Technology Rules:** 1. Begin with a defined customer or business outcome. 2.
Distinguish current systems from planned, experimental, and future capabilities. 3. Assign
business, system, data, and technical ownership. 4. Prefer the smallest adequate and governable
architecture. 5. Keep authoritative information and data sources identifiable. 6. Protect
security, privacy, accessibility, and continuity by design. 7. Preserve human accountability
for consequential outcomes. 8. Test integrations, recovery, exceptions, and failure
conditions. 9. Govern AI through approved data, defined tasks, validation, and human review.
10. Make vendor dependencies and exit paths visible. 11. Record material decisions, changes,
incidents, and learning. 12. Measure business and customer outcomes, not technology activity
alone. 13. Retire systems and data deliberately. 14. Scale only after evidence supports
expansion.

**Master Digital Governance Rules:** No material system without an owner. No material data
flow without purpose and authorization. No critical access without approval and review. No
high-impact automation without accountable human control. No customer promise without
operational support. No future capability represented as current. No critical continuity
assumption left untested. No hidden material change. No unresolved critical risk silently
accepted. No technology decision separated from its business consequences.

**Master Decision Framework — before approving a technology decision, answer:** 1. What
outcome is required? 2. What evidence supports the need? 3. What is the capability's verified
status? 4. Who owns the business outcome, system, data, and technical operation? 5. What
customer, operational, security, privacy, accessibility, and compliance effects exist? 6. What
dependencies and vendors are involved? 7. What alternatives were evaluated? 8. How will the
solution be tested and released? 9. What are the failure, rollback, recovery, and exit paths?
10. How will success and unintended effects be reviewed?

**Master Action Checklist:**
- [ ] Maintain a verified system and dependency register.
- [ ] Assign ownership for every material system and data domain.
- [ ] Keep current, approved, planned, experimental, future, and unknown states separate.
- [ ] Map critical customer journeys and data flows.
- [ ] Govern product information and content publication.
- [ ] Define data quality, access, retention, and lineage.
- [ ] Apply meaningful human review to AI-assisted work.
- [ ] Review access, security, privacy, backup, and continuity.
- [ ] Test critical restoration and incident processes.
- [ ] Assess material vendors and exit conditions.
- [ ] Govern releases and architecture changes.
- [ ] Measure customer and business outcomes.
- [ ] Preserve decisions, limitations, incidents, and learning.
- [ ] Review the future roadmap without converting vision into unsupported commitment.

**Part End Note (verbatim):** *"Muv's technology should make the company more capable without
making it less understandable. The strongest digital ecosystem is not the one with the
greatest number of tools. It is the one in which customer journeys are coherent, information is
trusted, data is governed, systems are owned, automation is responsible, recovery is possible,
and people remain accountable. The MUV Digital Flagship™ provides an owned centre for brand,
product, commerce, and customer experience. Data creates intelligence when its purpose,
quality, and limitations are known. AI creates value when it is grounded, reviewed, and
governed. MUV Universe™ creates direction when future ambition remains clearly separated from
present fact. Technology must continue to evolve. The principles in this volume ensure that
evolution strengthens Muv rather than surrounding it with uncontrolled complexity."*
