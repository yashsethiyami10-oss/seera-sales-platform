## 1. Source-mapping finding — disclosed prominently, not buried

A dedicated research pass across the full 81-chapter, 14-Part MUV Knowledge Library (verified
20,845 lines) found **no Part or chapter titled or substantively dedicated to "Growth &
Optimization Intelligence."** Specifically checked and ruled out:

- The full 81-chapter Master Index was read in full. No chapter beyond Part VII's own five
  (already Domain 2, frozen) discusses marketing growth mechanics, acquisition economics, or
  campaign experimentation.
- Part VII's own closing Part Summary ("MUV Marketing Playbook in One View") explicitly lists
  **"Growth"** as one of its 14 marketing areas — *"Build acquisition, retention, community,
  and expansion responsibly"* — confirming growth is a distributed theme already fully covered
  within Domain 2 (frozen), not a separate, unclaimed body of content.
- Direct keyword search for "growth loop," "conversion rate," "A/B test," "cohort," "retention
  curve," "funnel," "flywheel" found only matches already inside Domain 2's own Chapter 5
  (`KO-PM-CH5-*`) or Domain 3's already-frozen customer-repeat content — nothing new, nowhere.
- Direct keyword search for all nine Founder Original IP engine names (see §3) found **zero
  occurrences** anywhere in the Library.

**Conclusion, stated plainly:** Domain 7 contains **zero newly-transcribed source content**.
Every Knowledge Object in this domain is either a **citation-only** reference to already-frozen
Domain 2 content (`KO-PM-CH1-*`, `KO-PM-CH5-*`, both from Chapters authored under Part VII —
Marketing, Communication & Growth), organized under this domain's own growth/optimization
framing, or a **Gap Record** for a named Founder Original IP capability confirmed absent from
the source. This is not a shortfall in research effort — it is the accurate, verified state of
the source Library, disclosed per the Source Rule rather than papered over with invented
content.

## 2. What this domain actually is

A **thematic re-organization, not a new body of knowledge**: it collects every growth-,
measurement-, acquisition-economics-, and experimentation-facing Knowledge Object already
owned by Domain 2 (frozen) into one AI-retrievable domain framed around growth and optimization
— useful for a future AI agent or Founder query scoped to "how does Muv think about growth"
without needing to already know that answer lives inside Domain 2's Launch Architecture and
Marketing Decisions & Learning chapters. Per Zero Duplicate Knowledge, none of the cited content
is restated — every Knowledge Object here is explicitly `citationOnly: true` and points to its
owning KOID.
