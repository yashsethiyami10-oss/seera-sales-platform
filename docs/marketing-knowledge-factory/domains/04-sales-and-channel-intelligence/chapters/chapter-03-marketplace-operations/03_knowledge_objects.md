## KO-SC-CH3-011 — Chapter Governance Summary

- **Purpose:** Preserve the chapter's closing governance content.
- **Scope:** Common Mistakes, Best Practices, Action Checklist, Key Takeaways, Chapter Summary.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part VIII, Ch.38, closing subsections.
- **Outputs:** Consolidated do/don't reference; 12-item Action Checklist; 4 Key Takeaways;
  Chapter Summary.
- **Dependencies:** KO-SC-CH3-001 through 010.
- **Relationships:** Mirrors the established pattern.
- **Governance Rules:** No new rule invented.
- **Validation Rules:** All 12 checklist items in original order.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**Common Mistakes:** Creating duplicate listings for the same SKU. Treating platform inventory
as fully reconciled. Running deals without complete contribution analysis. Advertising an
out-of-stock or weak listing. Ignoring suppressed listings and policy notifications. Returning
questionable stock to sellable inventory without control. Reporting gross sales as net channel
performance.

**Best Practices:** Maintain one canonical listing register. Audit product, content, price, and
inventory together. Forecast campaigns with operations. Reconcile orders, inventory, returns,
fees, and settlements. Link review and return patterns to product and service owners. Assign
account-health ownership.

**Action Checklist:**
- [ ] Platform fit and economics assessed.
- [ ] Account ownership, access, and security controlled.
- [ ] Canonical SKU and listing identity aligned.
- [ ] Product, price, images, and content current.
- [ ] Inventory states reconciled.
- [ ] Replenishment plan approved.
- [ ] Order and fulfilment controls operating.
- [ ] Promotions use complete economics.
- [ ] Reviews, questions, returns, and claims routed.
- [ ] Settlements reconciled.
- [ ] Account health monitored.
- [ ] Quick-commerce expansion is locally ready.

**Key Takeaways:** Marketplace selling combines catalogue, inventory, fulfilment, pricing,
reputation, and finance. The listing, SKU, stock, order, return, and settlement must remain
connected. Displayed revenue is not realised contribution. Quick commerce requires local
operational readiness, not only listing access.

**Chapter Summary:** *"Marketplace Operations establishes the commercial and operational
controls needed to sell reliably through Amazon, Flipkart, quick commerce, and related
platforms."* Source's own transition line: *"Next: Marketplace Content & Conversion governs how
accurate commercial information becomes confident customer action"* — the sourced
justification for Chapter 4 following next.
