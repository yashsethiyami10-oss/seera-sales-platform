referenced, not restated. This is disclosed here, in Chapter 5's own README, and in the domain
audit's consistency check — not silently handled.

## Part VII (Domain 2, frozen) — channel-messaging content confirmed non-duplicative

Part VII (Domain 2's source) contains its own "channel" content (Ch.32's Channel-Specific
Strategy, Ch.34's Channel Marketing) — verified to answer "how do we **communicate** to/through
this channel?" (messaging, creative, POS/training assets), while Part VIII answers "how do we

channel operations. Marketing owns the communication system that supports them"*). This is a
genuine complementary relationship, cited where relevant, never duplicated.

## Chapter scope summary

1. **Pricing & MRP Architecture** (Ch.36) — sales philosophy, customer value, price
   architecture, MRP, channel economics, revenue responsibility, pricing governance.
2. **Sales & Distribution** (Ch.37) — sales process, lead/opportunity management,
   demonstrations, objections, follow-up, conversion, sales training, distribution
   architecture, partner management (including Institutional Partnerships and
   Distributor & Retailer Relationships), territory planning, market expansion.
3. **Marketplace Operations** (Ch.38) — Amazon/Flipkart/quick-commerce account governance,
   catalogue setup, inventory, orders, fulfilment, pricing, advertising interface, reviews,
   returns, settlements, performance controls.
4. **Marketplace Content & Conversion** (Ch.39) — listing philosophy, title/information
   hierarchy, images, search discoverability, product details, offers, conversion, reviews,
   reputation, experiments, cross-channel consistency.
5. **Commercial Decisions & Channel Evolution** (Ch.40) — revenue planning, forecasting,
   targets, incentives, commercial metrics, customer acquisition economics, territory
   intelligence, CRM (cited from Domain 3, not re-transcribed), channel evolution, expansion
   decisions, documentation, knowledge transfer, sustainable commercial leadership. Plus Part
   VIII's own closing Part Summary ("MUV Sales & Distribution System in One View").
