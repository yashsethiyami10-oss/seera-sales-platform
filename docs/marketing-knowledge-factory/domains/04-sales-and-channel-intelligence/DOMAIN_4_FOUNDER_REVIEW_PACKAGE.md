## 3. The domain's central discipline test: the CRM System cross-domain duplication risk

Domain 3 (frozen) had already imported a narrow excerpt of this same Chapter 40 — its "CRM
System" subsection (lines 9700–9721) — as `KO-CI-CH6-001` and `KO-CI-CH6-002`. This risk was
identified and disclosed in Domain 4's own README **before Chapter 1 was authored**, with an
explicit commitment: when Chapter 5 reached the CRM System subsection in its full-chapter
mirror, it would cite Domain 3's existing KOs rather than re-transcribe their content.

That commitment held. `KO-SC-CH5-008` is a **citation-only Knowledge Object** — it names the
subsection, confirms its real position in Chapter 40's sequence (between Territory Planning and
Sales Documentation), and points directly to `KO-CI-CH6-001`/`KO-CI-CH6-002`, both independently
re-verified present in Domain 3's live, frozen registry. No CRM content was duplicated anywhere
in this domain.
