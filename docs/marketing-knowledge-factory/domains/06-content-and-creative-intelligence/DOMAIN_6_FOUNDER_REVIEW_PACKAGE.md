## 1. Domain structure — a Founder-designated three-part merged domain

Domain 6 was explicitly authorized as a **merged domain** against an unusually large requested
topic list (brand communication, copywriting, messaging, headlines/hooks/CTA frameworks,
product storytelling, script writing, video, creative direction, image/banner strategy,
packaging communication, marketplace creative standards, social content, AI-assisted content
governance, creative scoring/review/quality frameworks, creative-visual-brand consistency). A
dedicated research pass, performed before any chapter was authored, sorted every requested topic
into exactly one of three categories:

- **Genuinely new content** — Part IV, "MUV Design System™" (Chapters 16-20), covering
  packaging, label, visual-asset, 3D/mockup, and design-control discipline. → **Chapters 1-5**.
- **Content already owned by a frozen domain** — messaging, storytelling, scripts, video,
  social content, marketplace listing standards, brand visual identity, and general AI
  governance all already exist, in full, in Domains 1, 2, 4, or 5. → **Chapter 6, citation-only,
  zero new source transcription.**
- **Content confirmed absent from the source Library entirely** — eight specific topics,
  verified by direct search, have no formal treatment anywhere in the Library. → **Chapter 7,
  Gap Records, zero source content, zero fabrication.**

## 3. Duplication risk — identified before Chapter 1 was authored, resolved by structure

Domain 6's duplication risk was structurally the highest of any domain built so far, given how
much of its requested scope overlaps four already-frozen domains. This was resolved not by
per-chapter ad hoc checks but by partitioning the domain itself: Chapters 1-5 contain only
content with no frozen-domain equivalent (verified section-by-section during research); Chapter
6 contains zero new source transcription by design; Chapter 7 contains zero source content by
design. Within Chapters 1-5, one placeholder cross-domain reference from Chapter 1
(`KO-CC-CH1-005`, originally a generic "Domain 1 Ch.14" note) was formally resolved to the
specific KOID `KO-BI-CH4-002` once Chapter 6's research identified it — Chapter 1's own files
were corrected in place, not left inconsistent.
