## KO-CC-CH3-001 — Product Truth Protection & Product Image System

- **Purpose:** Establish the Founder's product-truth constraint and preserve the eight-stage
  product image system.
- **Scope:** Product Truth Protection, Product Image System (Figure 3.1), complete.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part IV, Ch.18 (Product Truth Protection, Product Image
  System).
- **Outputs:** The Founder constraint; Figure 3.1 eight-stage flow; eight-row image-type table.
- **Dependencies:** None (chapter-opening KO).
- **Relationships:** governs KO-CC-CH3-002 through 009.
- **Governance Rules:** *"Do not change anything in the bottle, label, size, or height."*
  *"Visual enhancement may improve lighting, background, composition, sharpness, or context. It
  may not replace the real product with an attractive fiction."*
- **Validation Rules:** Figure 3.1's eight stages and all eight table rows preserved; the
  Founder constraint never softened.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:** The Founder repeatedly gave a direct constraint: *"Do not change anything in the
bottle, label, size, or height."* This is the first rule of product imagery. Visual enhancement
may improve lighting, background, composition, sharpness, or context. It may not replace the
real product with an attractive fiction.

**Figure 3.1 — Product Image System:** Truth image → hero image → benefit image → detail image
→ how-to image → lifestyle image → comparison image → brand-story image.

| Image Type | Primary Purpose |
|---|---|
| Truth image | Establish the accurate product reference |
| Hero image | Present the product clearly and attractively |
| Benefit image | Explain an approved benefit |
| Close-up image | Show label, texture, closure, or relevant detail |
| How-to image | Explain approved usage |
| Lifestyle image | Place the product in a realistic customer context |
| Before/after image | Show a truthful authorized comparison |
| Brand-story image | Connect the product to approved brand meaning |

## KO-CC-CH3-010 — Chapter Governance Summary

- **Purpose:** Preserve the chapter's closing governance content.
- **Scope:** Common Mistakes, Best Practices, Visual Asset Checklist, Key Takeaways, Chapter
  Summary.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part IV, Ch.18, closing subsections.
- **Outputs:** Consolidated do/don't reference; 10-item Visual Asset Checklist; 4 Key
  Takeaways; Chapter Summary.
- **Dependencies:** KO-CC-CH3-001 through 009.
- **Relationships:** Mirrors the established pattern.
- **Governance Rules:** No new rule invented.
- **Validation Rules:** All 10 checklist items in original order; the source's own "Visual
  Asset Checklist" title preserved, not renamed to "Action Checklist."
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content:**

**Common Mistakes:** Replacing a real label with generated text. Changing bottle proportions.
Showing unapproved benefits. Using low-resolution output for marketplace zoom. Inserting
unrelated products into a single-product story. Treating a beautiful image as accurate without
comparison.

**Best Practices:** Build every set from one truth image. Use a defined image sequence. Keep
one primary message per image. Compare generated output at high zoom. Verify the complete
listing as a system.

**Visual Asset Checklist:**
- [ ] Product matches the truth image.
- [ ] Label is unchanged.
- [ ] Bottle proportions are correct.
- [ ] Image purpose is clear.
- [ ] Claim is approved.
- [ ] Resolution and ratio suit the channel.
- [ ] Background is clean and realistic.
- [ ] Brand treatment is consistent.
- [ ] Product set contains no unintended items.
- [ ] Final output has been reviewed at full size.

**Key Takeaways:** Every product visual must remain faithful to the real approved product.
Different image types have different roles in the customer decision journey. Marketplace and
digital optimisation cannot override accuracy. AI can assist production, but it cannot become
the source of product truth.

**Chapter Summary:** *"Visual Asset Production exists to make the real product easier to
understand and more desirable without changing its truth. Accuracy is the foundation; premium
composition builds on top of it."* Source's own transition line: *"Next: 3D Visualization &
Mockups defines how concepts and applications may be simulated without becoming false
production records"* — the sourced justification for Chapter 4 following next.
