## KO-IS-001 — Institutional Sales Process & Lead Generation

- **Purpose:** Preserve the eleven-step sales journey framework and the ten lead sources as the
  process every institutional account moves through, with the pipeline-discipline rule.
- **Scope:** Sales Process (Figure 2.1), Lead Generation, complete — included as required
  supporting context, not distribution-channel content.
- **Inputs:** MUV Knowledge Library, Part VIII, Ch.37 (Sales Process, Lead Generation).
- **Outputs:** Figure 2.1 eleven-step framework; ten lead sources (including "institutional
  lists" as a named source).
- **Dependencies:** None (repository-opening KO).
- **Relationships:** governs KO-IS-002 through 007; provenance cross-reference to Marketing
  Knowledge Factory's `KO-SC-CH2-001` (same source content, independently mirrored).
- **Governance Rules:** *"A list is not a pipeline. Every lead requires source, relevance,
  owner, status, and next action."*
- **Validation Rules:** Figure 2.1's eleven steps preserved in exact order; all ten lead sources
  preserved.
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Founder Review Ready
- **Change History:** 2026-07-31 — created.
- **Evidence Classification:** Verified

**Content — Figure 2.1 — Sales Journey Framework:** 1. Identify a relevant market or account.
2. Generate or receive a lead. 3. Qualify fit, authority, need, timing, and economics. 4.
Understand the customer or partner context. 5. Present the relevant Muv value. 6. Demonstrate or
provide proof where appropriate. 7. Clarify questions and objections. 8. Agree commercial and
operational terms. 9. Confirm order, payment, supply, and responsibilities. 10. Fulfil and
support. 11. Follow up and learn.

**Lead sources:** marketing; website or marketplace; field prospecting; retailer/distributor
research; **institutional lists**; referrals; trade/community networks; events; customer
expansion; creator or campaign response.
