## 2. Muv AI Sutra™ — The Intelligence Constitution of Muv

- **File:** `Muv_AI_Sutra_Master_MASTER1.md`
- **Actual path:** `.claude/docs/MUV_Knowledge/Muv_AI_Sutra_Master_MASTER1.md`
- **Type:** Markdown (`.md`)
- **Size:** ~113 KB, ~4,200 lines
- **Declared identity:** "Muv AI Sutra™ — The Intelligence Constitution of Muv — Living Master —
  Version 1.1 Corrected (Canonical Draft)"
- **Role:** Standalone AI-domain constitution, separate from the Master Founder Edition above.
  Currently contains one volume — **Volume I — Foundation** — with 12 chapters (Why Muv Needs AI, Muv
  AI Philosophy, Muv AI Principles, Muv Knowledge System, Muv Intelligence Layer™, AI Roles Across
  Muv, AI Governance and Human Control, AI Risk Management, AI Security/Privacy/Data Protection, AI
  Ethics/Transparency, AI Learning & Continuous Improvement, AI Collaboration & Multi-Agent
  Intelligence).
- **Classification:** Master file for its own subject. Not a duplicate of Part XII (different
  depth/structure). **Relationship to Part XII is only partially declared:** the Sutra names "the MUV
  Knowledge Library™" 13 times as its own senior canonical source (§4.2–4.3, §5.4), but neither file
  states how the Sutra's 12 chapters map onto Part XII's 7 AI chapters specifically, and the Library
  never references the Sutra at all — see the audit report's Conflicts section (corrected during
  Phase 0 validation).
- **Approval status:** Self-labeled "Canonical Draft" — **not confirmed final/approved**. Treat as
  provisional until the Founder states otherwise.
