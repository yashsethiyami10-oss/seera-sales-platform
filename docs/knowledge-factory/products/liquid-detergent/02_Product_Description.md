**Content:**

MUV Liquid Detergent™ is a fabric-care liquid laundry detergent produced by MUV Care Co. as a
single product family offered in three fragrance/colour variants — **Lavender Garden**
(lavender colour, Lavender Eco fragrance), **Indian Rose** (pink/yellow colour, Rose Petal
fragrance), and **Cool Water** (blue colour, DM Comfort fragrance) — each available in **1 Litre**
and **5 Litre** pack sizes, for a total of six SKUs.

The three variants share one production formulation (see `03_Manufacturing.md`) and differ only
in colourant and fragrance, added at the same stage of the same 10-litre batch process. This is
a genuine single product family, not six independently formulated products — the Knowledge
Factory rule against duplicating enterprise knowledge across SKUs is honoured throughout this
package accordingly.
