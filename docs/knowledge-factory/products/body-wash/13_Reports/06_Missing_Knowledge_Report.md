## Highest priority

1. **Zero safety content of any kind** — the most severe gap of any of the ten product families
   audited this session, for a direct-skin-contact product.

   decision on whether this is an obsolete placeholder or an intended fourth product.

## What this means practically

Despite a lower raw N/A percentage than the three prior manufacturing-only-SOP products (see
`03_Knowledge_Object_Statistics.md`), this package carries the single most consequential
individual gap of the entire session: **the AI cannot answer any real skin-safety question about
MUV Body Wash™ with sourced information.** Real Founder input on the priority items above —
especially locating any real Safety Data Sheet or dermatological testing documentation that may
exist outside this repository — is strongly recommended before customer-facing launch, more so
than for any of the nine prior packages.
