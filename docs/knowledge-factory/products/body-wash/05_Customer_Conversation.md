# MUV Body Wash™ — Customer Conversation Flows

> 12 required conversation flows, same 8-field structure reused throughout this Knowledge
> Factory: Situation, Customer Goal, Care Goal, Opening, Guidance, What to Avoid, Escalation,
> Closing. Care Intelligence's Truth → Safety → Care → Clarity → Actionability → Validation
> sequence applies to every flow, with heightened caution against inventing cosmetic/
> dermatological claims throughout.

---

## KO-BW-CONV-001 — First-Time Customer

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Situation** | A customer new to MUV Body Wash™ asks a general "tell me about this" question. |
| **Customer Goal** | Understand what it is, which variant might suit them, and whether it's right for their skin. |
| **Care Goal** | Give a complete, honest picture — the real active ingredient and formulation facts, three variant fragrance families, and the real gaps (no sourced skin-type/usage guidance) — without a polished pitch that invents benefit claims. |
| **Opening** | Welcome the question directly. |
| **Guidance** | State the sourced purpose (salicylic-acid-active liquid body wash), the three variants and their sourced fragrance families, and the two pack sizes; be upfront that skin-type suitability and usage instructions aren't published yet. |
| **What to Avoid** | Inventing a benefit claim ("gently cleanses," "leaves skin soft") or a skin-type suitability claim. |
| **Escalation** | Not required for a routine first-time inquiry. |
| **Closing** | Confirm they have a realistic, honest picture. |

## KO-BW-CONV-006 — Sensitive Skin (only if verified — it is NOT, documented honestly)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Situation** | A customer with sensitive skin asks whether this product is safe for them. |
| **Customer Goal** | Avoid a product that could trigger irritation. |
| **Care Goal** | Per this task's explicit "only if verified" instruction: **no source verifies suitability for sensitive skin** — this flow exists specifically to document that honestly, not to assert a claim. |
| **Opening** | Take the concern seriously and directly — sensitive-skin questions deserve a careful, non-dismissive answer. |
| **Guidance** | State plainly that MUV has not published sensitive-skin suitability guidance for this product, and that this package's own source audit found zero safety content of any kind for this product (see `08_Safety.md`) — the honest answer is genuine uncertainty, not reassurance. Recommend the customer consult a dermatologist or perform their own patch test if they choose to proceed, framed as their own judgment call, not a MUV instruction. |
| **What to Avoid** | Claiming the product is "gentle," "hypoallergenic," or "suitable for sensitive skin" — none of these are sourced, and inventing any of them here would be a serious, high-consequence hallucination given the real absence of safety documentation. |
| **Escalation** | If the customer describes a real, current reaction, escalate immediately per KO-BW-DT-004. |
| **Closing** | Confirm the honest uncertainty was communicated clearly, without minimizing their concern. |
