# MUV Body Wash™ — Objection Handling

---

## KO-BW-OBJ-001 — "Is this safe for my skin?"

- **Confidence:** N/A — honesty-framed, given a genuine, severe sourcing gap
- **Response guidance:** Take the question seriously. State plainly that no safety or
  dermatological information is sourced for this product — the most significant gap in this
  package (`08_Safety.md`). Never reassure with an invented "yes it's safe" or "dermatologically
  tested" claim. If the customer has a real concern, recommend consulting a professional or
  performing a patch test as their own judgment call, not a MUV instruction.

## KO-BW-OBJ-002 — "Does it contain harsh chemicals like SLES?"

- **Confidence:** MEDIUM — sourced ingredient presence, no safety characterization
- **Response guidance:** Confirm SLES is a real, sourced ingredient (primary cleanser). Do not
  characterize it as "harsh" or "gentle" — neither characterization is sourced. Per the Knowledge
  Library's own governance rule (`00_Source_Register.md` §3), never claim "chemical-free" or
  "non-toxic," since no source supports such a claim and the governance rule explicitly forbids
  it.

## KO-BW-OBJ-003 — "Is this good for sensitive skin?"
