**Content:**

The 8-step order follows a generically typical acid-cleaner sequence: water/surfactant/thickener
charged first, acid added next (with an explicit caution), then colour and fragrance last.

**What is directly source-confirmed (HIGH confidence) — the single strongest rationale statement
found in any of the four SOPs audited this session:** Step 4's instruction states, verbatim,
**"Never add water into acid"** — this is not a generic inference this package is adding; it is
the SOP's own explicit, stated safety rationale for why HCl is added slowly into the pre-charged
water batch (acid-into-water, the safe order) rather than the reverse. See
`09_Safety_and_Risk.md` (KO-BC-SAFETY-001) for this instruction's full safety treatment.

**What is generic industry context, not MUV-confirmed (LOW confidence):**
- Why SLES/Acid Thickener are charged before HCl (typical sequencing for this formulation
  class, but not stated as the reason in the source)
- Why colour/fragrance are added last (standard practice, not stated as the reason)
