## KO-BC-SAFETY-001 — Manufacturing Safety (real, sourced, but minimal)

**Content:**

This SOP has **no dedicated safety section** (unlike Toilet Cleaner's 4-bullet section), but it
does contain **one real, explicit, directly-quoted safety instruction embedded in the process
step itself**: *"Never add water into acid."* This is a standard, well-known chemical-handling
principle (adding water to concentrated acid can cause violent exothermic splattering; the safe
order is acid-into-water) — and it is genuinely, verbatim sourced here, not generically inferred
the way this package handles unstated safety context for other products.

**What this one instruction does NOT cover, and this package does not extend it to cover:**
- PPE requirements (no gloves/goggles/mask instruction, unlike Toilet Cleaner's SOP)
- Ventilation requirements
- First-aid procedure
- Spill handling

**Not yet available (REQUIRES FOUNDER INPUT):** all of the above manufacturing-safety gaps.
