## KO-BC-MFG-002 — Manufacturing Process Steps

- **KOID:** KO-BC-MFG-002

- **Approval Status:** DRAFT — Pending Founder Review
- **Review Date:** Upon Founder approval
- **Source:** `MUV_Bathroom_Cleaner_Production_SOP_With_Photo.docx`

**Content — 8-step process, verbatim:**

| Step | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | Charge 8.5 L DM water into the mixing tank and start slow stirring. |
| 2 | Slowly add 200 g SLES. Mix for 8–10 minutes until completely dissolved. |
| 3 | Add 150 g Acid Thickener slowly. Mix for 10 minutes until uniform. |
| 4 | Very slowly add 1.2 L HCl into the batch while stirring continuously. **Never add water into acid.** Mix for 10 minutes. |
| 5 | Add pre-dissolved colour and mix for 2 minutes. |
| 6 | Add fragrance and mix for 3 minutes. |
| 7 | Continue mixing for 10 minutes until the product is smooth and homogeneous. |
| 8 | Transfer to the filling tank and fill into bottles. |

**Step 4 contains this package's clearest, most directly source-stated safety instruction across
all four product families audited** — "Never add water into acid" — see `09_Safety_and_Risk.md`.

**No in-process quality checkpoint (pH or otherwise) is documented at any step** — same absence
pattern as Toilet Cleaner (contrast with Dishwash Gel's real pH range).

**Order dependency:** per the same Knowledge Library governance principle cited in all three
prior packages: *"the exact addition order and process steps for...bathroom cleaner...must come
from an approved SOP"* — this 8-step order is treated as fixed.

**Not yet available (REQUIRES FOUNDER INPUT):**
- Mixing equipment specification
- Temperature control requirements
- Stirrer speed/type ("start slow stirring" is qualitative only)
