**Content — documented risks arising from real, confirmed gaps (not hypothetical product
hazards):**

1. **No manufacturing safety section exists** for a batch that handles Caustic Soda directly
   (KO-DW-SAFETY-001) — an operational/documentation risk, not a claim that the product itself
   is unsafe.
2. **No consumer safety data exists** for a hand-contact product (KO-DW-SAFETY-002).
3. **No numeric viscosity/foam/grease test method exists**, only qualitative QC language
   (KO-DW-QC-001) — a quality-consistency risk across batches, not a safety risk.
4. **Colour and Citric Acid Solution quantities are "as required"/"if required"**, not fixed
   quantities (KO-DW-MFG-001) — a batch-to-batch consistency risk if not tightly controlled by
   trained operators.
