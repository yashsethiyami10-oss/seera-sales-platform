## KO-DW-MFG-002 — Manufacturing Process Steps

- **KOID:** KO-DW-MFG-002
- **Title:** MUV Dishwash Gel™ — Process Steps & Mix Sequence
- **Category:** Manufacturing SOP
- **Tags:** [dishwash-gel, manufacturing, process, sop]

- **Relationships:** KO-DW-MFG-001, KO-DW-QC-001, KO-DW-THEORY-001
- **Owner:** MUV Knowledge Factory (pending Founder approval)
- **Approval Status:** DRAFT — Pending Founder Review
- **Review Date:** Upon Founder approval
- **Source:** `MUV_Dishwash_Liquid_Gel_Production_SOP.docx`

**Content — 13-step process, verbatim:**

| Step | Material/Action | Instruction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Water | Charge 8.6 L DM water and start stirrer. Mix 2 min. |
| 2 | EDTA | Add EDTA and dissolve completely. Mix 2 min. |
| 3 | Caustic | Prepare caustic solution separately and add slowly. Mix 3–4 min. |
| 4 | LABSA Slurry | Add slowly. Mix 5 min. |
| 5 | SLES | Add gradually to avoid excess foam. Mix 10 min. |
| 6 | CAPB | Add and mix 5 min. |
| 7 | CDEA | Add and mix 2–3 min. |
| 8 | Glycerine | Add and mix 2 min. |
| 9 | **pH Check** | Maintain pH 6.5–7.5. Raise with diluted caustic, lower with citric acid solution. |
| 10 | Preservative | Add Phenoxy Ethanol. Mix 5 min. |
| 11 | Colour | Add pre-dissolved yellow colour. Mix 2 min. |
| 12 | Fragrance | Add lemon fragrance. Mix 3 min. |
| 13 | Salt | Add salt solution slowly until desired thickness. Final mix 15 min. |

**This is the most complete in-process QC step of the three product families audited so far** —
see `08_Quality_Control.md` for the full QC section this pH check belongs to, including the
two-directional correction instruction ("raise with diluted caustic, lower with citric acid
solution") not present in either Liquid Detergent's or Toilet Cleaner's SOPs.

**Order dependency:** per the same Knowledge Library governance principle already cited for the
prior two product families (`# final MUV Knowledge Library™.txt`, line ~5553): *"the exact
addition order and process steps for dishwash liquid ... must come from an approved SOP"* — this
13-step order is treated as fixed, not a suggestion.
