**Content:**

MUV Dishwash Gel™ is a Home Care surfactant-based liquid dishwashing gel, sold as a single
lemon-fragranced, yellow-coloured formulation in three pack sizes — **500 ml**, **1 Litre**, and
**5 Litre**. Like Toilet Cleaner, this is a single-formulation product family (no named
fragrance/colour variants); unlike either prior product family, its Quality Control section is
the most complete found so far, with a real pH range (6.5–7.5) and named corrective actions in
both directions.
