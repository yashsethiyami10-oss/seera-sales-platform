**Content — verbatim acceptance criteria:**

- **Appearance:** Clear yellow gel
- **pH:** 6.5–7.5
- **Performance:** Good foam and grease cleaning
- **Phase stability:** No phase separation
- **Viscosity:** Desired viscosity (qualitative — no numeric target or test method stated)

**In-process pH correction (from `06_Manufacturing_SOP.md`, Step 9):** if outside 6.5–7.5, raise
with diluted caustic, lower with citric acid solution. This is a real, two-directional
corrective-action instruction — unlike Liquid Detergent (single pH target point, no correction
instruction) or Toilet Cleaner (no checkpoint at all).

- No colour-match tolerance or reference standard for "clear yellow"
- No sampling plan, frequency, or batch-release/hold procedure
- No test equipment specification (e.g. pH meter calibration)
