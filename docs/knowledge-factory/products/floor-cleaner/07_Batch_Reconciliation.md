calculation.

**Note on nominal batch size vs. summed quantity:** the SOP's title block states "Batch Size: 10
Litres." Summing the liquid components alone (9.8 L water + 0.65 L SLES + 0.06 L water-for-
silicone ≈ 10.51 L) plus roughly 254 g of solid additions (fragrance, Alfox 200, Phenoxy
Ethanol, colour, silicone emulsion) puts the total reasonably close to the nominal 10L label —
closer than the equivalent gap found in the Glass Cleaner package — but still not an exact
literal match, a normal SOP convention rather than an error.

**Illustrative unit yield** (informational only, not a production plan): at the sourced fill
weights, a 10,764 g batch would yield approximately **10.6 units of the 1L pack** (1015 g each)
or **2.1 units of the 5L pack** (5020 g each) before accounting for any real-world process loss —
these two figures are mutually exclusive scenarios (a real batch would be split by variant/pack
size, not produce both simultaneously), presented only to illustrate the raw arithmetic.
