- **KOID:** KO-FC-MFG-002

- **Approval Status:** CONFIDENTIAL — Manufacturing
- **Review Date:** Upon Founder approval
- **Source:** `MUV_Floor_Cleaner_Production_SOP_With_Product_Photos.docx`

**Content (verbatim, 8-step numbered procedure — Step 5 is the ONLY variant-specific line in the
entire SOP):**

1. Fill tank with 9.8 L DM water and start stirrer.
2. Add SLES slowly and mix 10 minutes.
3. Premix fragrance and Alfox 200, then add slowly. Mix 10 minutes.
4. Add Phenoxy Ethanol and mix 2 minutes.
5. **Add colour (Blue for Cloud Walk / Lavender for Velvet Mist).** ← Variant Inheritance
   override point — see `17_Variant_Inheritance_Map.md`.
6. Premix Silicone Emulsion with water and add slowly.
7. Mix for 20 minutes.
8. QC check and fill into bottles.

**No safety instruction appears anywhere in this procedure.** Unlike Bathroom Cleaner (one real
safety instruction) and even Glass Cleaner (zero, but at least a QC bullet list), this SOP has
neither a safety section nor a QC criteria list — Step 8's "QC check" is a bare instruction with
no defined criteria anywhere in the document.
