- **KOID:** KO-GC-MFG-002

- **Approval Status:** CONFIDENTIAL — Manufacturing
- **Review Date:** Upon Founder approval
- **Source:** `MUV_Glass_Cleaner_Production_SOP_With_Photo_Rev1.docx`

**Content (verbatim, 9-step numbered procedure):**

1. Charge 10 L DM water into the mixing tank and start slow stirring.
2. Add 125 g SLES slowly and mix for 8–10 minutes until fully dissolved.
3. Add 200 ml Butyl Cellosolve and mix for 5 minutes.
4. Add 800 ml IPA slowly while stirring continuously. Mix for 5 minutes.
5. Add 50 g Acetic Acid slowly. Mix for 3 minutes.
6. Add 10 g BKC and mix for 3 minutes.
7. Add pre-dissolved blue colour and mix for 2 minutes.
8. Add 10 ml fragrance and mix for 5 minutes.
9. Filter if required and transfer to filling tank.

**No safety instruction appears anywhere in this procedure** — confirmed by reading the complete
SOP body text. This contrasts with Bathroom Cleaner's Step 4 ("Never add water into acid"); no
equivalent caution exists in this SOP despite Step 5 (Acetic Acid) and Step 6 (BKC) both
involving materials with real handling considerations.
