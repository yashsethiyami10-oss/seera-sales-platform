**Content:**

| Material | Quantity (per 10L batch) | Generic Functional Role (widely-known, not MUV-specific) |
|---|---|---|
| DM Water | 10 L | Solvent / base carrier |
| IPA (Isopropyl Alcohol) | 800 ml | Solvent, fast evaporation, streak-reduction, degreasing aid |
| SLES | 125 g | Surfactant/cleaning agent |
| Butyl Cellosolve | 200 ml | Glycol ether solvent, grease-cutting/co-solvent |
| Acetic Acid | 50 g | Mild acid, mineral-deposit/hard-water-spot cleaning aid |
| BKC (Benzalkonium Chloride) | 10 g | Quaternary ammonium compound, commonly used as a surfactant/antimicrobial agent in cleaning formulations |
| Fragrance | 10 ml | Scent — **not named/identified** in the source |
| Blue Colour | 1 g | Colourant — **named**: "Ocean Blue" |

**Important abbreviation-expansion caveat (same discipline as all four prior packages):** "IPA"
and "BKC" are expanded here to their standard, generally-known chemical names (Isopropyl Alcohol,
Benzalkonium Chloride) because these are unambiguous, universally-recognized industry
abbreviations — this is not the same as inventing an INCI name, exact grade, concentration
percentage, or supplier, none of which are sourced and none of which are stated here.

**Notable difference from Bathroom Cleaner:** this is the first product in this session's four
prior packages plus this one where the **colour is named** ("Ocean Blue") while the fragrance is
not — a genuine mixed data-completeness state, not a full gap like Bathroom Cleaner's (where
neither was named) nor a full completeness like the earlier three products (where both were
typically named).

**Not sourced (REQUIRES FOUNDER INPUT):** full INCI names, exact chemical grades, concentration
percentages, supplier names, COA, SDS, and the specific fragrance identity/name.
