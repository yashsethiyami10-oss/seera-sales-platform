**Note on nominal batch size vs. summed quantity:** the SOP's title block states "Batch Size: 10
Litres," but the raw materials as listed sum to roughly 11.01 L of combined liquid volume (10 L
+ 0.8 L + 0.2 L + 0.01 L) plus 186 g of solid additions (SLES, Acetic Acid, BKC, Blue Colour) —
the "10 Litres" label is best read as a nominal/rounded batch-scale designation rather than a
literal sum of the listed quantities, a normal SOP convention. This is presented as a transparent
arithmetic observation, not an assertion that the SOP contains an error.

**Illustrative unit yield** (informational only, not a production plan): at the sourced fill
weight of 515 g/unit (`KO-GC-VAR-001`), an 11,196 g batch would yield approximately **21.7 units**
of the 500 ml pack, before accounting for any real-world process loss, filtration loss, or
filling-line variance (none of which are sourced).
