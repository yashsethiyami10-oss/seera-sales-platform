# MUV White Phenyl™ — Customer Conversation Flows

> 12 required conversation flows, same 8-field structure used throughout this Knowledge Factory
> (reused methodology, first established for Bathroom Cleaner — see
> `13_Reports/08_Knowledge_Reuse_Summary.md`): Situation, Customer Goal, Care Goal, Opening,
> Guidance, What to Avoid, Escalation, Closing.

---

## KO-WP-CONV-001 — First-Time Customer

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Situation** | A customer new to MUV White Phenyl™ asks a general "what is this" question. |
| **Customer Goal** | Understand what it is and whether it suits their floor-cleaning needs. |
| **Care Goal** | Give a complete, honest picture including real gaps. |
| **Opening** | Welcome the question directly. |
| **Guidance** | State the sourced purpose (milky-white, pine-oil-emulsion floor cleaner), the two confirmed pack sizes (1L, 5L), and be upfront that usage/dilution instructions aren't published yet. Clarify this is distinct from MUV Black Phenyl™ if relevant. |
| **What to Avoid** | Inventing a dilution ratio or usage method. |
| **Escalation** | Not required for a routine first-time inquiry. |
| **Closing** | Confirm the honest picture was useful. |
