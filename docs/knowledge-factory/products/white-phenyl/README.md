## Naming — real discrepancy, resolved the same way as three prior products

The Product Chart never uses the word "White" — it lists this product simply as **"MUV Phenyl"**
(rows 20–21, 1L/5L). The Production SOP consistently and exclusively calls it **"MUV White
Phenyl."** **This task's Founder Instruction — "MUV White Phenyl™" — matches the SOP exactly**,
resolving the naming question the same way established for Bathroom Cleaner's "Fresh," Glass
Cleaner's "Crystal," and Pure Bleach's "Pure." "MUV Phenyl" is preserved as the Chart's
legacy/shorthand name.

## Product identity — independently confirmed, not inferred

A prior audit (Black Phenyl) only *presumed* the Chart's "MUV Phenyl" rows corresponded to this
SOP. This package's own fresh audit **independently confirms** that identity: the Chart's two
pack sizes (1L, 5L) match the SOP's two pack sizes exactly, no other candidate "Phenyl" product
or SOP exists, and the formulations are visibly distinct from Black Phenyl (pine-oil, milky white
vs. black-phenyl-concentrate, black). See `00_Source_Register.md`.

## No pack-size conflict — unlike Black Phenyl
