**Content:**

MUV Toilet Cleaner™ is a Home Care acid-based toilet bowl cleaner produced by MUV Care Co.,
sold as a single formulation in two pack sizes — **500 ml** and **5 Litre**. Unlike MUV Liquid
Detergent™, this product is not offered in multiple named fragrance/colour variants: one blue
colourant ("Acid Blue Colour") and one fragrance descriptor ("Harpic Floral," per the source
SOP's own naming — see the note in `10_Product_Variants.md`) apply to both pack sizes.

This is a genuinely simple, two-SKU product family — the Knowledge Factory rule against
duplicating enterprise knowledge across SKUs is honoured by writing shared knowledge once here
and in `03`–`09`, with only pack-size-specific fields (net weight, MRP) repeated in
`10_Product_Variants.md`.
