# MUV Pure Bleach™ — Objection Handling

> Honest responses to common customer concerns/objections. Per Care Intelligence, Truth comes
> before Persuasion — no objection is handled by minimizing a real gap or inventing reassurance.

---

## KO-PB-OBJ-001 — "Bleach is harsh / damaging"

- **Confidence:** N/A — response is honesty-framed, not a safety claim
- **Response guidance:** Acknowledge the concern as reasonable — bleach-based products are
  commonly understood to require care. State the one real, sourced fact relevant to this concern
  (the explicit "do not mix with acids or ammonia" restriction) without asserting a broader safety
  or gentleness claim that isn't sourced. Do not claim the formulation is "gentle" or "safe for
  all surfaces" — neither is sourced.

## KO-PB-OBJ-002 — "I'm worried about the chlorine smell"

- **Confidence:** HIGH — directly sourced
- **Response guidance:** This is expected and normal — the SOP's own Quality Control criteria
  list "Characteristic chlorine odour" as a pass condition, not a defect. If the smell seems
  unusually strong or different from expected, that's worth a real product-quality report (route
  to support), not something the AI diagnoses itself.

## KO-PB-OBJ-003 — "Can I use it on coloured clothes?"

- **Confidence:** N/A — not sourced
- **Response guidance:** Be honest: no source confirms safety on coloured fabric. Do not say yes
  or no as a MUV-confirmed fact. General consumer caution (bleach products commonly affect colour)
  can be offered as ordinary, clearly-labeled general knowledge, not a MUV-specific claim — the
  customer should be encouraged to test on a small hidden area first if they choose to proceed,
  framed as their own judgment call, not a MUV instruction.

## KO-PB-OBJ-004 — "Is it safe around my kids/pets?"

- **Confidence:** N/A — not sourced
- **Response guidance:** Be honest that consumer/child/pet safety guidance isn't published for
  this specific product. Do not reassure with an unsourced "yes it's safe" — see `08_Safety.md`.

## KO-PB-OBJ-005 — "Why doesn't the description tell me how to use it?"

- **Confidence:** HIGH — an honest characterization of a real, confirmed gap
- **Response guidance:** Acknowledge directly: MUV's only source document for this product is a
  manufacturing SOP, not a consumer usage guide — this is a genuine content gap, not something
  being withheld. Encourage checking the physical product label once purchased, or offer to flag
  the question for follow-up.

## KO-PB-OBJ-006 — "How is this different from other bleach brands?"

- **Confidence:** N/A — no sourced differentiator exists
- **Response guidance:** Do not invent a competitive differentiator. State the real, sourced facts
  (sodium hypochlorite based, 500ml HDPE bottle, formulated for household cleaning/whitening)
  without claiming superiority to any named or unnamed competitor. The competitor scan
  (`00_Source_Register.md` §8) confirms MUV's own sources make no competitive comparison claim
  either — there is nothing to responsibly repeat here.

## KO-PB-OBJ-007 — "Can I use this instead of my regular toilet/glass/floor cleaner?"

- **Confidence:** MEDIUM — grounded in real product-comparison facts
- **Response guidance:** Redirect honestly per `04_Decision_Trees.md` KO-PB-DT-003 — MUV has
  purpose-built products for each of those surfaces with their own sourced formulations; MUV Pure
  Bleach™'s own sourced purpose is general household cleaning/whitening, not a stated substitute
  for those dedicated products.

## KO-PB-OBJ-008 — "Why is there no MRP shown / why does the price seem to change?"

- **Confidence:** HIGH — grounded in real, binding governance (`FR-001`/`FR-002`)
- **Response guidance:** Pricing is always retrieved live from the current Product Catalog, never
  stored in this Knowledge Package — this is a deliberate governance decision (Commercial/
  Knowledge Separation), not a data gap or inconsistency. See `10_LIVE_DATA_MAPPING.md`.
