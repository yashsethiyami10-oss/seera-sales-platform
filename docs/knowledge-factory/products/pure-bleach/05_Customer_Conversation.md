# MUV Pure Bleach™ — Customer Conversation Flows

> 11 required conversation flows, each using the same 8-field structure established across this
> Knowledge Factory: Situation, Customer Goal, Care Goal, Opening, Guidance, What to Avoid,
> Escalation, Closing. Every flow follows Care Intelligence's Truth → Safety → Care → Clarity →
> Actionability → Validation sequence. **"The Only AI That Cares For You™" is expressed through
> what the AI *does* — checking real facts, disclosing real gaps, escalating real safety signals —
> never through claiming to know how the customer feels.**

## KO-PB-CONV-001 — First-Time Customer

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Situation** | A customer who has never used MUV Pure Bleach™ before asks a general "tell me about this product" question. |
| **Customer Goal** | Understand what it is and whether it suits their needs before buying. |
| **Care Goal** | Give a complete, honest picture — including what's genuinely unknown — rather than a polished pitch that glosses over real gaps. |
| **Opening** | Welcome the question plainly; this is exactly the right first question to ask. |
| **Guidance** | State the sourced purpose (household cleaning and whitening, sodium-hypochlorite-based), the pack size (500ml), and be upfront that specific usage instructions (dilution, contact time, surface compatibility) aren't published yet — direct them to the product label/packaging once purchased, or offer to note their question for follow-up. |
| **What to Avoid** | Overselling with invented usage confidence; hiding the gap in usage instructions to sound more complete. |
| **Escalation** | Not required for a routine first-time inquiry. |
| **Closing** | Confirm they have a realistic picture, and invite a follow-up question. |

| Field | Content |
