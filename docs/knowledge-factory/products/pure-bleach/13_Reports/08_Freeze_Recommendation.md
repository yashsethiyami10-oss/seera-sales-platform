# MUV Pure Bleach™ — Freeze Recommendation

## Recommendation: **CONDITIONAL FREEZE**

This package is recommended for freeze **as a documentation artifact** — its process quality is
100/100 (see `06_Product_Quality_Score.md`), every governance rule was followed, and it is
internally consistent and fully validated. It should **not** be treated as a customer-ready,
comprehensive product guide until the Founder addresses the priority gaps in
`05_Missing_Knowledge_Report.md`, given:

1. **First aid guidance is entirely unsourced** for a hypochlorite-based product — the single
   highest-consequence gap of any product family this session.
2. **Consumer usage instructions do not exist in any source** — the AI cannot currently tell a
   customer how to use this product, only what it's made of and how it's manufactured.
3. **48.4% of this package's Knowledge Objects are explicit "Unknown" markers** — the highest
   proportion of any of the seven product families built this session.

## What "freeze" means here

Freezing this package as-is means: it becomes the authoritative Product Knowledge Package for MUV
Pure Bleach™, the AI uses it exactly as documented (including all its honest "Unknown, defer/
escalate" answers), and it is not further modified except by explicit Founder instruction — the
same discipline applied to all six prior frozen packages. It does **not** mean the underlying
knowledge is complete; the gap register remains open and actionable.

## Recommended next Founder actions (not required before freeze, but before customer-facing launch)

- Supply or commission real first-aid guidance (ideally from an actual product SDS/label).
- Supply consumer usage/dilution/contact-time instructions.
- Confirm shelf life and the numeric available-chlorine QC specification.
- Confirm surface/fabric compatibility guidance.

## Comparison to the six prior packages at their own freeze point

Every one of the six prior packages also froze with real, disclosed gaps (Founder Input
Registers ranging from ~30 to 39 categories) — this is consistent with that established pattern,
not an anomaly. What's different here is the *proportion* of gaps relative to the source
material's total scope, driven by the SOP being manufacturing-only rather than a fuller document
like several prior products' SOPs.

## Stop Rule

Per the Founder's explicit instruction: **STOP.** Do not begin the next Product Family without
explicit Founder approval.
