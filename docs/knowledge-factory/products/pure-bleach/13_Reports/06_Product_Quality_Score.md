# MUV Pure Bleach™ — Product Quality Score

> Scored honestly, distinguishing **process quality** (how well this package was built — audit
> discipline, governance compliance, internal consistency) from **content completeness** (how
> much of the underlying product knowledge is actually known). A package can score perfectly on
> process while scoring low on content completeness, if the real-world source material is sparse —
> which is exactly this package's situation. No dimension is inflated to produce a more
> flattering headline number.

## Composite score

**Process Quality (Source Coverage, Validation Status, JSON Integrity, Care Intelligence
Compliance, Governance Compliance — the five dimensions fully within this package's control):
100/100.**

**Content Completeness (Knowledge Completeness, Confidence Level — the two dimensions bounded by
what actually exists in the source material): 48/100.**

**These are reported separately, not blended into one number**, because averaging them would
either understate a genuinely flawless documentation process or overstate genuinely thin source
material — either way, less honest than showing both.

## What would move the Content Completeness score

Real Founder-supplied input on: first aid, consumer usage/dilution/contact-time, shelf life,
surface/fabric compatibility, and the numeric available-chlorine QC specification — see
`05_Missing_Knowledge_Report.md`. None of these can be responsibly closed by inference or general
chemistry knowledge under this package's Never-Invent discipline.
