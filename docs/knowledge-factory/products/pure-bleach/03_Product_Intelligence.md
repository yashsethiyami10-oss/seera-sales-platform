## KO-PB-INTEL-015 — Frequently Misunderstood Use Cases

- **KOID:** KO-PB-INTEL-015
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM — built from a real, sourced cross-product safety fact, not invented
- **Evidence:** SOP §7 ("Do not mix with acids or ammonia-based cleaners"); cross-referenced
  against other MUV product families' own sourced formulations (`fresh-bathroom-cleaner/
  06_Manufacturing_SOP.md`, `toilet-cleaner/03_Manufacturing.md`, `crystal-glass-cleaner/
  06_Manufacturing_SOP.md` — all three real, previously-sourced products contain Acetic Acid or
  another acid-class ingredient)
- **Source:** Cross-package fact comparison, both sides independently sourced

**Content:** A customer might reasonably assume it's fine, or even more effective, to use MUV
Pure Bleach™ together with — or right after — an acid-based MUV cleaner (Toilet Cleaner,
Bathroom Cleaner, Glass Cleaner all contain a sourced acid ingredient) to "boost" cleaning power.
This is exactly the scenario the SOP's own mixing restriction exists to prevent. This is real,
useful product intelligence built from two independently sourced facts (this SOP's mixing rule,
plus the other products' own sourced acid content) — not an invented claim about either product.
See `04_Decision_Trees.md` for how this becomes an escalation rule.
