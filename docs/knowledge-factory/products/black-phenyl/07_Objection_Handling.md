# MUV Black Phenyl™ — Objection Handling

## KO-BP-OBJ-001 — "Phenyl products are harsh on floors"

- **Confidence:** N/A — honesty-framed, not a safety claim
- **Response guidance:** Acknowledge the concern as reasonable. State the sourced formulation
  facts (emulsifier, surfactant, cleaning/disinfecting base) without asserting a "gentle" or
  "safe for all floors" claim — neither is sourced.

## KO-BP-OBJ-002 — "I'm worried about the strong phenyl smell"

- **Confidence:** HIGH — directly sourced
- **Response guidance:** This is expected — the SOP's own QC criteria list "Characteristic black
  phenyl fragrance" as a pass condition, not a defect. If the smell seems unusually strong or
  different from expected, that's worth a real product-quality report, not something the AI
  diagnoses itself.
