## Sequence applied: Truth → Safety → Care → Clarity → Actionability → Validation

Every one of the 13 Customer Conversation flows and 8 Objection Handling entries follows this
order explicitly:

1. **Truth first** — no flow states an unsourced fact as confirmed. Most consequential example:
   KO-BP-CONV-007 (Hospital) and KO-BP-CONV-013 (Hygiene-Related Queries) both explicitly refuse
   to assert a disinfection efficacy claim, kill-rate, pathogen list, or regulatory certification
   — none are sourced, even though the product's own formula table calls it a "disinfecting
   base," a fact tempting to overstate.
2. **Safety before Care** — child-safety (School) and food-safety-adjacent (Restaurant) contexts
   both surface the real, honest gap (no consumer safety guidance sourced) proactively rather
   than waiting to be asked.
3. **Care without false reassurance** — no flow claims the product is "safe around kids/pets" or
   "safe on any floor" because nothing says otherwise.
4. **Clarity** — KO-BP-CONV-012 (Odour Control) explicitly distinguishes the real, sourced
   fragrance characteristic from an unsourced odour-elimination performance claim — two different
   things, only one of which is real.
5. **Actionability** — every flow ends with a concrete next step: an honest answer, a referral to
   institutional sales, or a real escalation.
6. **Validation** — cross-checked against `04_Decision_Trees.md` and `08_Safety.md` for
   consistency.
