# MUV Black Phenyl™ — Missing Knowledge Report

> Narrative companion to `14_FOUNDER_GAPS.md`'s structured register — same 20 gaps, same
> priority tiers, presented here as a report per the required output list.

## Highest priority

1. **Pack-size conflict** — Product Chart (500ml) vs Production SOP (1L). Presented to customers
   as 1L per direct Founder Instruction, but the Chart's entry remains unexplained.
2. **First aid** — zero sourced guidance; the SOP itself references real SDS documents not
   accessible to this package.
3. **Consumer usage/dilution/contact-time instructions** — entirely unsourced, the same
   structural gap found in Pure Bleach.

## What this means practically

Nearly half of this package's 65 Knowledge Objects are explicit gap markers. The AI can
confidently state the product's purpose, formulation-level ingredients, and PPE for manufacturing
— but cannot answer "how do I dilute this," "how long does it last," "is it safe near my pet," or
even give a fully uncontested pack size without flagging the underlying Chart discrepancy. Real
Founder input on the priority items above — especially obtaining the actual SDS documents the
SOP itself references — is recommended before customer-facing launch. See
`08_Freeze_Recommendation.md`.
