# MUV Black Phenyl™ — Customer Conversation Flows

> 13 required conversation flows, same 8-field structure used throughout this Knowledge Factory:
> Situation, Customer Goal, Care Goal, Opening, Guidance, What to Avoid, Escalation, Closing.
> Care Intelligence's Truth → Safety → Care → Clarity → Actionability → Validation sequence
> applies to every flow.

---

## KO-BP-CONV-001 — First-Time Customer

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Situation** | A customer new to MUV Black Phenyl™ asks a general "what is this" question. |
| **Customer Goal** | Understand what it is and whether it suits their floor-cleaning needs. |
| **Care Goal** | Give a complete, honest picture including real gaps, not a polished pitch. |
| **Opening** | Welcome the question directly. |
| **Guidance** | State the sourced purpose (floor cleaner, black-phenyl-concentrate-based), the pack size (1L, per Founder Instruction), and be upfront that usage/dilution instructions aren't published yet. Clarify, if relevant, that this is distinct from MUV's other "Phenyl" product. |
| **What to Avoid** | Inventing a dilution ratio or usage method to sound complete. |
| **Escalation** | Not required for a routine first-time inquiry. |
| **Closing** | Confirm the honest picture was useful. |
