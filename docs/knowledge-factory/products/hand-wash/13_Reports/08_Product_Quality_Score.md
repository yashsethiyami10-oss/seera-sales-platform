> vs. content completeness, reported separately, with the Safety Risk Flag pattern (originated

---

## ⚠️ Safety Risk Flag (read before the numeric scores)

This package's raw Knowledge Completeness and Confidence Level scores are numerically *higher*
than every prior manufacturing/formulation-only-SOP product this session, including Body Wash —
but this reflects an unusually precisely sourced formula, process, and colour/availability
dataset for 4 variants, **not** that this product is better documented for safe, repeat, direct
hand contact. **Zero safety content exists for any of the six `FR-005` mandatory fields** — the
same severity of gap found for Body Wash (which directly triggered `FR-005`), now for a product
used on hands, potentially multiple times daily. **Additionally, Life Shield's antibacterial/
protective status is unconfirmed** — a real risk of customer or future-content misunderstanding
that this package actively investigated and could not resolve. The scores below must be read
alongside `14_FOUNDER_GAPS.md` priority items 1–2, not instead of them.

## What would move the score in a way that actually matters

Real Founder-supplied safety/first-aid/shelf-life documentation and a real answer on Life
Shield's antibacterial status would move both the numeric score AND the Safety Risk Flag —
closing the gap that matters most here is not the same as closing the gap that would raise this
number the most (e.g. adding a container-material fact would raise Knowledge Completeness without
addressing the real risk).
