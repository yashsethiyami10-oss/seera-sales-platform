# MUV Hand Wash™ — Care Intelligence Report

> Truth → Safety → Care → Clarity → Actionability → Validation, applied throughout.

## Where this discipline mattered most

- **KO-HW-CONV-008 (Antibacterial / Protective Claim Inquiry)** — the highest-risk conversation
  in this package. Truth first: no source confirms an antibacterial claim for Life Shield. Never
  hedges toward "probably" or "helps protect." This is the clearest test case this session of
  `FR-005`'s named risk (a "Shield"-type name implying an unverified claim).
- **KO-HW-CONV-007 (Safety / Skin-Sensitivity Inquiry)** — honest disclosure of a real, total
  content gap rather than a generic reassurance. Directs to a healthcare professional rather than
  inventing guidance.
- **KO-HW-CONV-005 (Availability Inquiry)** — a new form of Care Intelligence discipline: honest,
  non-defensive handling of "why can't I get X in Y size," explicitly avoiding the misleading
  "out of stock" framing for something that was never offered.
- **KO-HW-SAFETY-010** — documents the Life Shield investigation's negative result (no source
  found) as a first-class finding, not a shrug — this is the Care Intelligence "Truth" step
  applied to an absence, not just a presence.

## Never Invent discipline, extended per `FR-005`

Dermatological, antibacterial, and skin-safe claims are all explicitly named as forbidden without
a source. This package tested that discipline against a real, tempting case (Life Shield's name)
and held the line throughout `02_Product_Architecture.md`, `04_Decision_Trees.md`,
`05_Customer_Conversation.md`, `07_Objection_Handling.md`, and `08_Safety.md`.

## Comparison to Body Wash

Body Wash's highest-discipline case was Sensitive Skin (KO-BW-CONV-006), documenting honest
uncertainty. Hand Wash extends the same discipline to a new category of risk — a plausible-
sounding but unverified functional claim implied by a product name, not just a general safety
question. This is a meaningfully different (and arguably harder) test of Care Intelligence than
any prior package this session.
