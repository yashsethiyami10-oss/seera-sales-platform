## Safety Critical (`FR-005`) — all six mandatory fields unsourced

Usage, Safety, Contraindications, First Aid (×3: eye/skin/ingestion), Storage, Shelf Life — every
one is `Unknown — Founder Decision Required`. This is the single largest content gap in the
package by field count, and the most consequential given repeat, direct hand contact.

## Life Shield antibacterial/protective claim — explicitly unconfirmed

No source assigns this property. Flagged as Critical because the risk of an assumed claim (from
the name alone) is real and specifically named by `FR-005`.

fragrance-note detail, container material, SKU codes/barcodes, packaging dimensions, formal
positioning statement, manufacturer name, disposal guidance — none of these are sourced.

pack size, and colour per variant are all HIGH-confidence, directly sourced facts.
