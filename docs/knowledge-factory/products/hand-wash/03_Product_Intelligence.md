## KO-HW-INTEL-005 — Manufacturing Process (Parent, shared except Steps 9–10)

- **Confidence:** HIGH — sourced exactly, 13 steps
- **Evidence:** SOP §3
- **Source:** Same SOP

**Content:**

1. Tank Preparation — clean, dry tank/stirrer/utensils
2. Water Charging — 7.2L DM water, slow agitation
3. SLES Addition — slow, side-of-tank, avoid fast mixing (minimize foam), mix until clear
4. CAPB Addition — slow, mix 8–10 minutes
5. CDEA Addition — slow, mix until uniform
6. Glycerin Addition — mix 5 minutes
7. Preservative — mix thoroughly
8. Pearl Paste — slow, mix until pearl effect uniform
9. **Colour** (variant-specific — never premixed with fragrance)
10. **Fragrance** (variant-specific — mix gently 10–15 min, never premixed with colour)
11. Viscosity Adjustment — salt solution, gradual, to required thickness
12. Quality Check — appearance (smooth & pearl finish), pH 5.5–6.5, viscosity stable, no
    lumps/separation
13. Filling — after foam settles, tighten pumps/caps, clean bottles, apply labels

**Two variant-specific steps (9, 10)** — see `02_Product_Architecture.md` KO-HW-INHERIT-001 for
the full inheritance treatment.
