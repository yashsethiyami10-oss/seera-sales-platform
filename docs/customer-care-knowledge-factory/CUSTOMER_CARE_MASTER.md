## Repository framing — read this before the Knowledge Objects below

Before authoring a single object, this repository's actual scope was investigated against the 4
already-frozen repositories, exactly as the Cross Repository Rule requires ("never duplicate
Knowledge Objects... preserve KOIDs, authority, and repository ownership"). The finding: **the
natural source for "Customer Care" — MUV Knowledge Library Part IX, "MUV Customer Experience™"
(Chapters 41–45) — has already been mirrored in full**, as Marketing Knowledge Factory's **Domain 3
(Customer Intelligence)**, 51 Knowledge Objects under the `KO-CI-` prefix. Domain 3's own README
records this directly: Part IX is "about service delivery, support operations, complaint recovery,
and loyalty/retention" — i.e., exactly Customer Care.

This means the Customer Care Knowledge Factory contains **zero fresh mirrored source content** —
the same honest outcome Marketing KF's own Domains 7 and 8 reached when their requested scope
turned out to already be owned by a frozen sibling domain. What this repository actually is:

1. **15 Citation-only Knowledge Objects** — organizing and pointing to real, already-frozen content
   across Marketing KF Domain 3, Institutional Sales KF, Founder Intelligence KF, and Product KF,
   into the specific Customer Care topic structure the Founder named, without re-transcribing any
   of it.
2. **6 Founder Decision Required Gap Records** — for topics the Founder explicitly named
   (Returns, Replacement, Refund, Warranty, a literal built Escalation Matrix, Customer Happiness
   as a distinctly defined concept) that were verified, with line-cited evidence, to have **no
   dedicated policy content anywhere in the source Library** — several places in the Library
   explicitly defer such policy to "approved channel policy" or state a claim "requires its own
   approved operational basis," rather than defining it. This is a real, confirmed absence, not an
   assumption — see each Gap Record's own Evidence field.
3. **1 Repository Governance Summary.**
